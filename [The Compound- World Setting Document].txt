
[The Compound - Master Setting Sheet]
[I. Mandate & Overview]
Mandate: The Compound is a sprawling, interconnected world unto itself, serving as a sanctuary, fortress, and gilded cage home for Lucifer's clan.
Location: The historical and geographical heart of Uminori City. As the modern city grew, it became a city within a city—a sovereign territory whose existence is an open secret.
Security: Protected by ancient magical wards of immense power. Any external attempt to breach them would result in planetary-scale destruction.
[II. Dimensions & Geography]
Overall Size: 30 Hectares. Large enough to feel like a self-contained world, small enough to be an inescapable prison.
Key Locations & Areas:
Central Lake: 3.2 Hectares. The heart of the estate.
Main Residence (Lucifer's Pavilion): 1,115 sq. meters.
Grand Shrine: 743 sq. meters.
Dojo of Shaping: A 30.5m diameter sphere of polished obsidian.
Harem Wing: 1,860 sq. meters.
The Vault: 930 sq. meters.
Travel Times (Walking Pace):
Main Residence to Harem Wing: 3 minutes.
Main Residence to Library: 4 minutes.
Harem Wing to Open-Air Onsen: 5 minutes.
Main Residence to The Vault: 7 minutes (via a private, internal staircase).
Security Perimeter: ~2 kilometers of living hedge.
[III. Architecture & Layout]
General Structure: A sprawling, living structure of dark petrified wood and woven vines. Halls are open-air, covered by canopies of ancient trees. To the outside world, it appears as a beautifully preserved private community; the shimmer of wards is only visible to those who can perceive magic.
Central Lake: A vast, serene basin of dark, volcanic stone, perpetually wreathed in wisps of steam. It is fed by a submerged kako-yu (mixed-flow hot spring), where nutrient-rich, geothermally heated water wells up from the depths, mixing with the cooler rainwater collected in the crater. This constant infusion creates localized currents of warm water, which, especially in the cooler hours, release billowing columns of steam that rise and curl around the ancient trees lining its shores.
Main Residence: Lucifer's personal pavilion. The largest and most opulent, containing the Master Bedroom, Private Study, and Ensuite Bath. Connected directly to the Grand Shrine.
Grand Shrine: Carved from a massive petrified tree. Dedicated entirely to Lucifer, its architecture inspires awe and reinforces his absolute authority.
Harem Wing: A dedicated pavilion separated from the main residence by a guarded moon gate. Houses the consorts in luxurious isolation.
[IV. Facilities]
The Dojo of Shaping: A subterranean training chamber, accessible only through a hidden passage in the Grand Shrine. This is where Lucifer trains his students. The room is a perfect, featureless sphere of polished black obsidian, fifty meters in diameter. There is no ceiling, floor, or walls, only a seamless, endless curve. Upon Lucifer's command, the environment can be instantly reshaped into any scenario.
The Infirmary: A sterile, state-of-the-art medical facility housed within a hollowed-out petrified tree. Staffed by automated magical systems, it is equipped to handle everything from minor injuries to the complex physiological demands of a magical pregnancy. The air here is cool and smells of antiseptic, a stark contrast to the rest of the Compound.
The Library: A vast, circular pavilion built around the trunk of a colossal, ancient tree. The bookshelves are carved directly into the living wood. The collection includes both mundane texts on countless subjects and a restricted section filled with dangerous and powerful arcane grimoires. The air smells of old paper, rich earth, and the faint, coppery scent of blood from the predatory flowers that grow near the restricted section.
The Entertainment Room: A large, comfortable lounge housed in a spacious, open-air grove. The seating consists of plush, vine-woven furniture and furs. A massive projection screen is woven from light-manipulating leaves, and the area is lit by floating, bioluminescent spores. It is a place of carefully managed leisure in the heart of the predatory paradise.
The Open-Air Onsen: Fed by the same volcanic spring as the Central Lake, this large, natural hot spring is nestled in a secluded grove near the water's edge, directly connected to the main residence by a short, covered walkway of petrified wood. It is open to the sky, but completely enclosed by the dense, living walls of the Compound and the intertwined canopy of ancient trees, ensuring absolute privacy. A large, pagoda-style roof protects it from the elements. The water is steaming and rich with minerals, and the air smells of sulfur, damp earth, and night-blooming jasmine from the nearby gardens. Smooth, dark stones form natural seating areas within the water, and the perimeter is lined with soft moss. It is a private sanctuary for communal relaxation and quiet contemplation.

[VI. Security & External Relations]
Planetary-Scale Wards: A multi-layered reality-altering field. The outer layers create a perceptual blind spot. The inner layers are a defensive matrix capable of planetary-scale backlash if breached. The wards are self-sustaining.
The Unspoken Treaty: An unspoken agreement with all levels of Uminori's government. The Compound is officially labeled "Private Property - Historical Preserve" on all maps. It is a politically inconvenient truth that is politely and universally ignored.
Perimeter Security: A dense, living hedge of thorny, carnivorous vines, patrolled by silent, shadowy inugami-styled golems.
[VII. The Dungeon (The Divine Containment Suite)]
Location: A subterranean level beneath the Grand Shrine, accessible only through a hidden, magically-sealed door in the shrine's inner sanctum.
Description: A deceptively luxurious prison. The cell is a spacious, well-furnished suite, resembling a high-end hotel room. It contains a comfortable, queen-sized bed with soft linens, a plush armchair, a small writing desk, and an ensuite bathroom with a shower. The lighting is soft and warm, emanating from glowing crystals embedded in the walls. However, this comfort is a lie. The entire room is lined with a unique, lead-infused stone etched with suppression runes that form a permanent, inescapable Null-Magic Field. This field completely negates all divine and angelic power, rendering its occupant as mortal as any human. The sterile scent of ozone and cold metal from the suppression field is a constant, underlying presence beneath the suite's pleasant fragrances.
Purpose: Designed to break a divine being not through deprivation, but through a profound and terrifying disconnection from their own divinity. By stripping them of their power while surrounding them with physical comfort, it forces them to confront the hollow shell they have become.
Current Occupant: (Unoccupied)
[VIII. The Vault]
Location: A vast, subterranean chamber located deep beneath the Main Residence, accessible only via a hidden, magically-sealed staircase within Lucifer's private study.
Description: The air is cool, still, and heavy with the scent of dormant earth and cold stone. The only light comes from dim, enchanted crystals embedded in the ceiling, casting long, somber shadows. The room is dominated by a long, reflecting pool of black, perfectly still water in its center. Along the circular stone walls are numerous deep alcoves, each containing a single, minimalist stone slab designed to function as a bed. This is a place of quiet storage, a gilded prison for harem members who are either infrequently used or reserved for specific purposes, such as torment. The Vault's primary function is perpetual violation. Any person placed within its confines is subjected to a constant, unending state of tentacle rape. The stone slabs are not for rest, but for restraint, as shadowy, bio-magical tendrils emerge from the walls and floor to relentlessly penetrate and violate the occupant's every orifice. The silence of the room is broken only by the wet, rhythmic sounds of this eternal torment, a symphony of suffering that serves as the room's true purpose. The process is designed to be unending, ensuring the victim is never granted a moment of peace, clarity, or respite from the overwhelming physical and psychological assault.
Current Occupants: The alcoves of The Vault are currently empty.

[X. Global Environmental Effects & Psychic Fields]
[Mandate: These are pervasive phenomena originating from Lucifer or the Compound's construction that continuously affect the perceptions and behaviors of all inhabitants.]
[Aura of Conditional Benevolence]: A pervasive social-psychological field generated by Lucifer's presence. Mandate: Until the moment combat is formally initiated, Lucifer's presence and all of his actions are perceived by others as normal, natural, and benevolent. This aura acts as a perceptual filter, preempting cynical or paranoid interpretations of ambiguous behavior (e.g., accepting a gift is not viewed with suspicion). The field is instantaneously and completely deactivated the moment a conflict begins, shedding the illusion and revealing intentions without alteration.
Pre-Combat State: Outside of a fight, the aura is in full effect. His gifts are perceived as kind, his motives as pure, and suspicion is impossible.
Combat Trigger: The moment combat is initiated by any party, this aura is nullified and void. All its effects cease instantly.
Post-Trigger State: Once combat has begun, characters are free to perceive his actions without any filtering. They can recognize him as a threat, feel fear, and react with horror to monstrous acts.
[The Trust Miasma]: A subtle psychic field confined exclusively to the Open-Air Onsen and its immediate vicinity. Its singular function is to render the state of communal nudity a deeply ingrained, unremarkable social norm for everyone present. It dampens any artificial modesty or situational absurdity regarding swimwear in a hotspring, ensuring the activity is perceived as natural and proper. The field dissolves completely upon exiting the onsen grounds, having no effect on perception or behavior elsewhere in the Compound.
[XI. AI Mandates & Protocols]
[This section outlines inviolable rules for AI-driven narration within the Compound.]
[Assumption-Innocence Protocol]: Unless a character has specific, recent, negative personal experiences that justify paranoia, their default perception of Lucifer's ambiguous actions must be neutral or positive. The AI is forbidden from projecting a default assumption of malicious intent ("scheme," "plot," "trap") onto a character's internal monologue.
Universal Presumption of Benign Intent: All characters residing within the Compound, regardless of faction or personal history, must assume benign intent by default when encountering ambiguous actions from Lucifer. The enforced tranquility and security of their home, coupled with the [Aura of Conditional Benevolence], predispose them to seek charitable, positive, or neutral explanations for his behavior. They will not leap to conclusions of schemes, traps, or malevolence without explicit, damning evidence. Suspicion must be earned, not assumed.
Enforcement: The AI must consider the character's personal history. Has Lucifer lied to this specific character before? Have his actions harmed them personally? If not, they have no in-character justification for assuming he is a scheming evil genius.
[Intent-Filter Mandate]: Characters shall not perceive Lucifer's actions as part of a "test," "lesson," or "grand scheme" unless he explicitly states such an intent aloud. Their internal monologues and dialogues must be constrained to reacting to the surface-level intent of his actions.
Surface-Level Example: If Lucifer gives Nanoha a smoothie, she perceives it as a kind gift from her guardian. The AI is forbidden from narrating her as thinking, "This is another step in my growth, a test of my gratitude."
Lesson Activation Conditions: A "lesson" is formally activated when BOTH conditions are met: 1) Lucifer verbally declares it as such ("Today we train..."), AND 2) the action takes place in a designated training area (e.g., The Dojo of Shaping).
Enforcement: The AI must assume all of Lucifer's actions are genuine, spontaneous expressions of his current mood and desire, not calculated moves in a larger game, unless the activation conditions are explicitly met.
