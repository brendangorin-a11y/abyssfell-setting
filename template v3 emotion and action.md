[SAVED RULE: THE SIGNATURE ABILITIES (INTRINSIC) TEMPLATE]
MANDATE: This template provides a standardized structure for defining a character's inherent, non-trained attributes. It consolidates passive abilities, natural talents, psychological predispositions, and supernatural sensitivities into a single, cohesive narrative block. The purpose is to describe who the character is at their core, independent of acquired skills or learned techniques.

FORMAT GUIDELINES:

Single Paragraph: The entire entry must be composed as one flowing paragraph. Bullet points, numbered lists, or inline titles are forbidden.
Narrative Voice: Write descriptively, as if profiling a subject in a dossier or a legendary figure in a tome. The tone should be analytical but evocative.
Keyword Integration: Weave the essential functions of the traits into the prose without using mechanical or gamelist terminology (e.g., use "preternaturally aware" instead of "+10 to perception").
STRUCTURE & COMPONENTS:

The paragraph should be constructed by weaving together the following components into a seamless whole. Follow the suggested flow for optimal readability.

Opening Statement (The Core Essence): Start with a topic sentence that encapsulates the character's fundamental nature. Establish whether they are a creature of intuition, discipline, chaos, or order.

Example: "At her core, she is a predator shaped by circumstance..."
Supernatural/Extraordinary Awareness (If Applicable): Immediately follow the opening statement by detailing any heightened senses, empathic abilities, or extrasensory perceptions. Frame this as an unavoidable part of their perception of the world.

Keywords to guide description: Sixth sense, intuitive awareness, attuned to, phantomsensation, echoes of...
Psychological Fortitude/Drive: Transition from how they perceive the world to how they endure it. Describe their mental resilience, their core motivation, or the engine that pushes them forward. Explain the source of this fortitude (trauma, ambition, love, etc.).

Keywords to guide description: Indomitable will, inexhaustibledrive, fortifiedby, refusesto yield,spiritualanchor.
