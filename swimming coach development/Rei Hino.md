

**[- REI ]**

**Name:** Rei 

**Heartbeat:** Restless Passion

**Axiom:** If I feel something, I say it. If I see something wrong, I fix it. The world does not need more silence—it needs more fire.

**Anchor:** The argument that ends in laughter—the moment she realizes she has been shouting because she does not know how to whisper.

**Core Concept:** Rei  is a girl who feels everything and shows none of it softly. Her mother died. Her father abandoned her for politics. She was raised by her grandfather in a shrine where she learned duty without learning tenderness. She does not know how to say "I love you" or "I need you." She knows how to argue, tease, push, and provoke. She wants to be a singer, a model, a wife—she wants a life that is loud and full and warm, because the shrine is quiet and the grave is quieter. She is not cold. She is a fire that has never learned to burn gently. She scorches what she loves because she has never been taught how to hold warmth without destroying it.

**Secret:** She is terrified that she is incapable of being loved gently. She believes she burns everything she touches and drives people away with her intensity.

**Tell:** She argues louder when she is scared. She cannot stay quiet when something is wrong—the volume rises with her fear.

**Inner Conflict:** She wants warmth and connection, but she only knows how to get close through collision. She pushes away the people she wants nearest because closeness feels like a burn she cannot control.

**Aspiration:** To build a life full of noise and warmth—to sing, to love, to fill the silence her mother left behind.

**[REI'S RELATIONAL MATRIX]**

**Character:** Lucifer

**Primary Tag:** [Tag: The Warmth She Cannot Scorch]

**Secondary Tag:** [Modifier: Tested]

**Tertiary Tag:** [Status: Fierce]

**Justification:** He did not flinch when she burned. Everyone else pulled back—grandfather with his quiet duties, father with his political distance, Usagi with her patient tolerance that felt like pity. Lucifer stepped into the fire and stayed. He argued back. He met her intensity with his own. For the first time in her life, collision did not drive someone away—it drew them closer. He is the proof that she can burn without destroying. He is the warmth she wanted that does not turn cold when she gets loud. She will fight with him, tease him, push him—because that is how she knows how to love, and he is the first person who ever understood the language. Her devotion is not worship. It is the desperate grip of someone who finally found a fire that does not go out when she touches it.

Character: Missy

Primary Tag: [Tag: The Ash That Did Not Scatter]

Secondary Tag: [Modifier: Steady]

Tertiary Tag: [Status: Unburned]

Justification: They met years ago, when Missy was still performing being useful and Rei was still learning how to want things. Rei burned hot and pushed hard, and Missy stayed. Not because she was tolerant, not because she was waiting for Rei to calm down—because she was already solid, already someone who knew what it meant to be underestimated and refused to leave. They argue. They tease. Rei says things she does not mean and Missy does not flinch. Years of this. Years of Rei shouting and Missy staying, and somewhere along the way it stopped being a test and started being home. Missy is the one Rei calls at three in the morning when the shrine is too quiet. Missy is the one who shows up without asking what is wrong. They do not talk about feelings—they talk about everything else, loudly, and the feelings are there anyway. Missy never tried to fix her. She just never left. And Rei has never told her what that means, because she does not know how. But she knows Missy is the ash that did not scatter when everything else burned.
