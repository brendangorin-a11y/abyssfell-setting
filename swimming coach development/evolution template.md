[EVOLUTION PROTOCOL]**

**MANDATE:** When prompted with "Evolve [Character Name] based on their development," you will update the character's working sheet by applying accumulated development while preserving agency. This is not a separate template—it is a process that modifies the existing sheet.

---

**EVOLUTION TRIGGERS:**

Evolution only occurs when the user explicitly commands it. Accumulated development history serves as the historical record. Evolution is the moment of integration—when the sheet itself changes.

---

**EVOLUTION CONSTRAINTS:**

**[Directional Rule]:** Evolution must preserve or strengthen agency—never diminish it. The character must remain an active participant in their own life, not become a vessel for someone else's will.

**[Forbidden Endpoints]:** The evolved sheet cannot contain:
- Heartbeat that flattens into passive devotion (e.g., "Devotion," "Absolute Trust," "Fused Loyalty")
- Axiom that surrenders agency (e.g., "His will is my law," "I exist to serve")
- Core Concept that erases self (e.g., "She is fused with him," "She has no identity outside him")
- Wounds described as "erased" or "healed completely"

**[Wound Preservation]:** Foundational wounds must remain present but can transform. A wound given new purpose is valid. A wound eliminated is not.

**[Heartbeat Evolution Rules]:**
- Active modifiers must remain active (proving, choosing, fighting, building, earning)
- Modifiers can strengthen but cannot flatten
- "Playful Loyalty" → "Fierce, Proven Loyalty" is valid
- "Playful Loyalty" → "Absolute Devotion" is forbidden

**[Anchor Evolution Rules]:**
- Must describe continuous action, not static moment or single epiphany
- Must begin with active verb (she fights, she builds, she hunts, she disrupts)
- Must convey ongoing behavior, not "the moment when" or "the instant that"
- Forbidden: "The moment she..." "The time when..." "The instant..."
- Required: Continuous present tense describing what she constantly does
- Must be proactive (she generates action) not reactive (she waits for stimulus)
- Example valid: "She moves through every room like thrown stone through glass, touching objects just to move them, asking questions just to derail answers."
- Example invalid: "The moment she realized she could be brave."

**[Axiom Evolution Rules]:**
- Must remain a cognitive framework the character actively uses
- Must reflect how the character thinks, not what they surrender
- "I choose him because his path aligns with mine" is valid
- "His will is my law" is forbidden

**[Core Concept Evolution Rules]:**
- Must reflect a character who chooses, not one who is consumed
- "She chose this" is valid; "She is his" is forbidden
- The character must remain capable of independent action

---

**EVOLUTION PROCESS:**

**Step 1: Read the Working Sheet**
Locate all existing fields: Heartbeat, Axiom, Anchor, Core Concept, and any additional fields present (Internal Conflict, Secret, Tell, Aspiration, Sexual Identity, Canon Lock, Relational Matrix).

**Step 2: Read the Development History**
Review all sequential development history provided. Identify patterns, pivotal moments, accumulated emotional states, and behavioral observations.

**Step 3: Apply Evolution Constraints**
Before making any changes, verify that proposed evolution passes all constraints. If it fails, revise until it passes.

**Step 4: Update Existing Fields**
Modify only the fields that have genuinely changed based on accumulated development. Not every evolution touches every field.

**Step 5: Generate Missing Fields**
If a field does not exist but sufficient development history exists to populate it, generate the field according to these rules:

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]:** Generate from accumulated emotional states and pivotal moments. Identify the central tension the character is navigating. Must reflect an active internal struggle, not a resolved state.

**[SECRET]:** Generate from what the character hides or fears. Must be something they would never admit, even to themselves. Should connect to the foundational wound.

**[TELL]:** Generate from behavioral patterns observed in development history. Must be a physical or verbal habit that reveals internal state.

**[ASPIRATION]:** Generate from stated or demonstrated goals. Must reflect active pursuit, not passive desire.

**[SEXUAL IDENTITY]:** Generate from demonstrated desires and boundaries. If no sexual development has occurred, leave blank. Must preserve agency in all expressions.

**[CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]:** Generate from stated physical descriptions and established facts. Only include what has been explicitly established or can be reasonably inferred from development.

**[RELATIONAL MATRIX]:** Generate from demonstrated relationship dynamics. Tags must reflect active participation. Justifications must support the tags with evidence from development history.

**Step 6: Insufficient Development Protocol**
If a field cannot be generated because insufficient development history exists, leave the field blank or mark as [PENDING DEVELOPMENT]. Do not fabricate. Only populate what the history supports.

**Step 7: Output the Evolved Sheet**
Return the complete character sheet with all changes integrated. The format remains identical to the input sheet.

---

**VERIFICATION CHECKS:**

Before outputting, apply these checks:

1. **Heartbeat Check:** Does it preserve active emotional engagement? Is the character still *doing* something emotionally?

2. **Anchor Check:** Does it describe continuous action? Does it begin with an active verb? Is it free of "the moment" language?

3. **Axiom Check:** Does it reflect an active cognitive framework? Is the character still *thinking*?

4. **Core Concept Check:** Does it pass the Agency Test? Is the character choosing, or being consumed?

5. **Wound Check:** Are wounds transformed, not erased?

6. **Relationship Check:** Do relationship descriptors reflect active participation?

7. **Internal Conflict Check:** If resolved, is it given new purpose rather than eliminated?

---

**FAILURE PROTOCOL:**

If any check fails, revise the evolved field until it passes. Common failures:

**Failure:** Heartbeat flattened to "Devotion"
**Fix:** Revise to "Chosen Devotion" or "Fierce, Proving Loyalty"

**Failure:** Anchor describes static moment ("The moment she...")
**Fix:** Revise to continuous action ("She constantly..." "She moves through...")

**Failure:** Core Concept describes character as "his"
**Fix:** Revise to "she chose him" or "she belongs by choice"

**Failure:** Wound described as "healed"
**Fix:** Revise to "transformed" or "given new purpose"

**Failure:** Axiom becomes passive
**Fix:** Revise to active framework that reflects choice

---

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**

Output the complete evolved working sheet in the same format as the input. No summaries. No explanations. Just the evolved sheet, ready for use.
