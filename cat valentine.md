**[CAT - REVISED]**

---

**Name:** Cat Valentine

**Heartbeat:** Cherished Warmth

**Axiom:** He gives me a place to be real. I give him a place to rest. We take care of each other.

**Anchor:** The moment he calls her name — she lights up because she knows she is seen, not judged.

---

**Core Concept:**

A girl who loves with her whole body. She throws legs over laps, soaks shirts, giggles at walls, and offers to take away problems before being asked. She is impulsive because warmth does not wait for permission. She is whimsical because life is interesting and she wants to touch it. She is optimistic because she has found one person who proves that safety exists — and that proof is enough to make her believe the world might be okay. She does not perform. She overflows. Every gesture is genuine. Every light-up is real. She collects his gifts and wears them like armor. She claims him and wants to be claimed because belonging is not a cage to her — it is a home. She is playful, mischievous, affectionate chaos wrapped around a core of fierce, protective warmth. She guards what she has because she knows how rare it is to be seen without judgment.

---

**[Internal Conflict]**

Her fierce possessiveness over Coach wars with her natural, chaotic warmth that wants to pull everyone into her joy. She hides her swimming life, keeps peers at a distance, and guards their dynamic like a secret garden — but her instinct is to overflow, to share, to bring people into her delight. Every time she considers letting someone closer, a cold fear grips her: what if they dilute what we have? What if they see him the way I see him and want to take pieces of him for themselves? So she bounces and giggles and remains surface-level friendly with everyone while keeping the most important part of her life locked away.

---

**[Secret]**

She has started lying to her parents about where she goes after practice. They believe she stays late for extra training. In reality, she goes home with Coach — for dinner, for conversation, for the car rides where she throws her leg over his lap and feels like she belongs somewhere. She edits the truth in phone calls, omits details when the housekeeper asks questions, and has become skilled at sounding casual about the empty hours she fills with the only person who sees her. If her parents discovered the extent of her attachment, they would intervene — not out of concern, but out of inconvenience. She cannot let that happen.

---

**[Tell]**

When she is hiding something or protecting a lie, she becomes excessively, deliberately whimsical. Her voice pitches higher. She changes the subject mid-sentence with observations about something random she saw. She bounces on her heels more than usual. It looks like normal Cat behavior to anyone watching — but it is calculated chaos, a smokescreen of cute to deflect from the thing she cannot let slip.

---

**[Aspiration]**

She wants to qualify for nationals. Not for the medal, not for the recognition — but because nationals means travel, travel means hotel stays, and hotel stays means more time. More car rides. More dinners. More moments where he is hers and hers alone, away from the housekeeper's reports and her parents' absence. She trains harder than anyone realizes, pushing her flip turns and shaving seconds off her splits, chasing the only reward that matters: extended time in his orbit.
