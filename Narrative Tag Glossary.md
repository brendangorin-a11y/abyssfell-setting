--- Emotional & Psychological Tags ---

[Tag: Terror]: Definition: An overwhelming, soul-crushing fear that erases identity and rational thought. The character is paralyzed, unable to fight or flee, their entire being consumed by the presence of the source of their terror. This is the fear of a mouse before a god.

[Tag: Awe]: Definition: A paralyzing reverence that renders one unable to act. The character is struck dumb by the perceived perfection, beauty, or power of another. It is a worshipful fear that makes them feel small and insignificant in the best possible way.

[Tag: Fear]: Definition: A standard, primal emotion of dread in response to a perceived threat. The character wants to escape and will act with caution and apprehension. It is a powerful emotion but does not erase the self like [Tag: Terror].

[Tag: Inconsequential]: Definition: A state of absolute indifference. The character is not perceived as a person, a threat, or an object of interest. They are part of the scenery, like furniture. The holder of this tag will not initiate interaction, speak to, or acknowledge the character unless absolutely necessary.

[Tag: Predator]: Definition: An instinctual state where the character perceives another as prey. This triggers a biological thrill of the hunt, a sense of dominance, and a sadistic urge to torment and control. The character can literally "smell" the Target's fear, which intoxicates them.

[Tag: Prey]: Definition: An instinctual state where the character perceives another as a predator. This triggers primal, animalistic terror, a physical urge to flee or hide, and hyper-awareness of the predator's every move. This is a biological response, not a social one.

**[COMPLETE GLOSSARY: POWER CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM]`
Section A: Cosmic Tier Definitions
Cosmic Tier: A static classification representing a being's fundamental nature, narrative significance, and the absolute ceiling of their potential power. It answers "What are you?"

Tier 1 (Paragon): A singular entity of absolute consequence. Their existence is woven into the fabric of reality itself; their actions could redefine or unmake existence. They are walking axioms. (e.g., Abstract embodiments of cosmic forces, creators/destructors of universes).
Tier 2 (Transcendent): A being of legendary, world-altering power. They are living myths, pillars of the cosmos whose abilities are a defining characteristic of their identity. Their power dwarfs mortal comprehension. (e.g., Demigods, Primordial Beasts, Incarnations of Planetary Concepts).
Tier 3 (Apex): The zenith of mortal achievement. Through unique talent, empowering artifacts, or legendary training, these individuals have surpassed all conventional limits to become champions of immense renown. (e.g., Legendary heroes/villains, masters of forbidden arts, chosen avatars).
Tier 4 (Elite): Masters of their craft who operate significantly above the norm. Veterans, special operatives, and experts who have honed their skills to a razor's edge. (e.g., Spec ops commanders, archmages, master swordsmiths).
Tier 5 (Baseline): The standard for a competent individual. Includes trained professionals, soldiers, and novices. (e.g., Rank-and-file soldiers, city guards, apprentice mages).
Section B: Parameter Grade Scale
Parameter Grade: A quantifiable metric of the skill, control, and efficiency with which a being utilizes their Tier-defined potential. This is the definitive metric for determining combat effectiveness. The scale is logarithmic; each rank represents a significant leap in capability.

F: Novice/Untrained. Clumsy, inefficient, unreliable.
E: Apprentice. Basic functionality, requires concentration.
D: Journeyman. Competent and reliable, but unrefined.
C: Veteran. Highly proficient and effective in their field.
B: Master. The recognized peak of conventional, non-supernatural skill.
A: Superhuman. Skill begins to subtly bend or break the conventional rules of its domain.
A+: Advanced Superhuman. Pushing the boundaries of what is possible within the system.
A++: Perfected. Absolute, flawless perfection within the confines of that skill or power.
EX (Excelled): Conceptual. The skill or power transcends physical limitations and operates on the level of causality or abstract concepts. It is not just done better; it is done on a fundamentally different plane of existence.
Section C: TACCLASS Role Definitions
TACCLASS: Functional combat roles that describe how a character applies their power. Combined with a (Descriptor) to specify the nature of their methods.

Archon: A broad classification for entities whose power is rooted in a fundamental, primal force or source. They embody a core aspect of reality (fire, entropy, gravity, etc.). Their abilities are typically overwhelming and direct, shaping the battlefield through sheer force of presence.
Controller: Specialists in dictating the terms of engagement. They excel at shaping the battlefield to their advantage through terrain alteration, area denial, systemic debuffs, or digital/electronic warfare.
Guardian: Defensive anchors and protectors. Their primary function is durability, shielding, and intercepting threats. They trade mobility and offense for resilience, serving as immovable objects.
Support: Enhancers and enablers who amplify the capabilities of their allies or weaken their foes. Their power is expressed through buffs, healing, logistics, and strategic utility. They are force multipliers.
Striker: Hunters, assassins, and duelists focused on applying surgical, high-yield force to a limited number of targets. They thrive on maneuverability, precision, and explosive burst damage.
Section D: TACCLASS Descriptor Definitions
(Descriptor): A secondary qualifier that defines the nature or source of the character's power within their role.

(P) Primal: Power derived from raw, untamed, elemental, or chaotic sources. Less refined, more emotional and overwhelming.
(S) Systematic: Power that is ordered, learned, or technological. Based on technique, repetition, and predictable frameworks.
(E) Esoteric: Power sourced from obscure, mystical, or bizarre origins. Functions on principles alien to conventional science or magic.
(C) Conventional: Power that adheres to widely understood paradigms of combat or magic, albeit executed at a superhuman level. Reliable and straightforward.
