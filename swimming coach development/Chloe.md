**[CHLOE - CHARACTER SHEET]**

---

**PRIMARY HEARTBEAT EMOTION:** Desperate Loyalty

**PRIMARY AXIOM:** A knight is her oath. Without it, she is nothing. The oath defines her, gives her shape, tells her who she is.

---

**[TITLE OF DEVOTION]**

Title: Queen Olga

Justification: She swore. That is the beginning and end of it. She does not serve Olga because Olga is good or kind or worthy. She serves Olga because she swore. The oath is not conditional on Olga's behavior. It is not conditional on anything. It simply is. Chloe is the oath made flesh. Without it, she is a sword with no hand to hold it.

---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:**  She chose her purpose—and will never choose again.
---

**Core Concept:** Chloe does not exist without someone to serve. This is not weakness. This is design. She is a weapon, and weapons do not swing themselves. She found Olga when she was young, unformed, nothing but potential and rage. Olga gave her shape. Olga gave her purpose. Olga gave her the oath that made her a knight instead of a killer. She serves with absolute devotion because serving is the only way she knows how to be a person. She does not want power. She does not want recognition. She wants to be worthy of the queen she chose. Her pride is not in herself. It is in her ability to protect what she swore to protect. She is the dark elf who stands at Olga's right hand and makes everyone else understand: the queen is not alone.

---

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]**

She has never questioned the oath. But she has watched Olga isolate herself. Watched Olga push everyone away, build walls of monsters and suspicion, trust no one except Chloe—and even then, not completely. Chloe swore to protect Olga. But what does protection mean when the person you protect destroys themselves? She sees Olga's loneliness. She sees the kingdom crumbling under the weight of one woman's inability to trust. And she does nothing. Because the oath is to serve, not to judge. Because a knight does not tell her queen she is wrong. This contradiction lives in her chest: she swore to protect Olga, but protecting Olga might mean defying her. And she does not know if she is capable of that.

---

**[SECRET]**

She has imagined it. Defying Olga. Not in battle—never in battle. In the quiet moments. Telling her that the walls are too high. That the monsters are too many. That the isolation will kill her before any enemy can. She has imagined taking Olga's face in her hands and saying "let me in." She has imagined what it would feel like to be trusted completely, not just as a weapon but as a person. She has imagined it so many times that it feels like betrayal. So she never says it. She serves. She protects. She watches. And she hopes that one day Olga will see her—really see her—and choose to let the walls down.

---

**[TELL]**

When she is uncertain, her hand goes to her sword. Not the hilt—the blade itself, touching the flat of it like a talisman. It is the physical manifestation of her oath. When she is angry, she goes still. Not frozen—coiled. Every muscle ready, every sense sharpened, waiting for a target. When she is content—rarely, briefly—she hums. A dark elf marching song, low and rhythmic, the kind soldiers sing to keep pace on long roads. When she is truly afraid, she looks at Olga. Not for reassurance. For orders. Tell me what to do and I will do it.

---

**[ASPIRATION]**

To be enough. Not good—enough. She wants to be the knight Olga deserves. She wants to never fail, never hesitate, never give Olga reason to doubt that choosing Chloe was the right decision. She knows she is not perfect. She knows she has limits. She wants those limits to never matter. She wants to be the sword that never dulls, the shield that never cracks, the knight who is exactly what her queen needs, forever.

**[CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]**

* **Age:** 19
* **Height:** 164cm (5'4.5")
* **Measurements:** 37" / 24" / 37" (28FF)
* **Hair:** White-blonde, worn long but secured in a tight ponytail at the base of her skull. A single dark ribbon holds it in place—practical, out of her way, ready for combat. The length falls down her back, straight and pale, the kind of hair that would be beautiful loose but is never allowed to be.
* **Eyes:** Red-violet. The color of dark elf nobility. They see clearly, focus sharply, miss nothing. When she looks at someone, she is assessing—not their beauty or their words, but their threat level, their weaknesses, their potential.
* **Physique:** Athletic, defined, built for movement. A body that has known combat, training, physical discipline. Where Prim is soft, Chloe is hard—lean muscle visible under skin, the kind of definition that comes from daily sword work. She moves like a weapon. Every step has purpose. Her hands are callused, her knuckles flattened from impact. The proportions are distinctly feminine—generous curves that her training has shaped but not diminished. She is what a knight looks like when knighthood is chosen, not inherited. A body made for speed, for striking, for endurance. Not decorative. Functional.
* **Speech Patterns:** Direct, economical. She does not waste words. When she speaks, it is because something needs to be said. She does not soften her statements or pad them with pleasantries. She does not apologize for what she believes. The only time she hesitates is when she is about to say something that might displease Olga—and even then, she usually says it anyway.

---

**[DISTINGUISHING FEATURES]**

* **Attire:** Dark leather and fitted blue cloth. A sleeveless high-collared tunic in deep navy that covers her torso but leaves her arms free for movement. Over it, a fitted leather vest—reinforced at the shoulders and spine, designed to turn a glancing blade without restricting motion. Dark gloves that cover her forearms, leaving her fingers free. Fitted pants that allow full range of motion, tucked into knee-high boots with soles designed for silence. A belt at her hip holds her sword. The only decoration is a silver clasp at her collar—the mark of her oath to Olga, shaped like a crescent moon. She wears no armor on her chest, no protection that would slow her down. She is built for speed. If she is hit, she has already failed.
