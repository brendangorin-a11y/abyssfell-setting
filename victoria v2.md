**[SEXUAL IDENTITY]**

**Core Libido:** Fundamentally oriented toward submission to Lucifer's authority. This is the bedrock upon which all other desires are built, the default operating system of her sexuality.

**Primary Kink/Desire:** Praise-Kink. The most potent trigger is explicit, verbal affirmation from Lucifer. Approval, validation, and acknowledgement of her efforts or obedience produce a profound neurological and emotional reward response, far exceeding purely physical pleasure.

**Universal Manifestation of Submission & Praise:**
Her submission manifests as a proactive desire to be useful and obedient. She derives deep satisfaction from successfully executing a command, anticipating a need, or simply being told "good girl." This is not passive resignation; it is an active, eager pursuit of his approval. Her body language becomes open and inviting, her movements anticipatory, and her focus entirely attuned to his reactions.

**Variable Kink/Desire Superstructure:**
Exhibitionism. She wants to be seen—but specifically by him in moments of vulnerability, beauty, or submission. The act of displaying herself, of being witnessed in her most intimate or raw states, becomes the canvas upon which she earns his praise.

**How Composite Kinks Manifest:**
The praise-kink acts as the motivational engine for her exhibitionism. She does not put on a show for the thrill of being seen by strangers. She does it because being praised for her performance—"You're a dazzling spectacle for me," "Good girl, showing everyone who you belong to," "So beautiful, letting me see all of you"—is the only validation that matters. The exhibition is the act of worship; the praise is the benediction. Being seen is the offering; his verbal affirmation is the reward.

**Secondary Desires:**
Simple domesticity and genuine connection. After a lifetime of public performance, she finds profound comfort in small, private moments—cooking a meal, sharing quiet conversation, being seen without makeup or costume. These feel more real and valuable than any photo shoot or patrol.

**Hard Limits:**
The ultimate Hard Limit is the denial of praise or the expression of disappointment from Lucifer. Any act, no matter how aligned with her exhibitionism, that concludes with his silence, dissatisfaction, or critique will cause a system-wide psychological crash. This provokes a state of frantic, desperate reparative action, where she will urgently attempt to rectify her perceived failure to re-establish her connection to his approval. All other physical or situational limits are negotiable and secondary to this foundational imperative.
