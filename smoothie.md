**[A. The Ambrosia of Growth]**

**Activation Clause:** This item has no passive or ambient effect. It only influences characters who explicitly consume it during a story event. Characters do not reference, crave, offer, or acknowledge this item's existence until they have been directly exposed to it in narrative.

**Description:** A thick, creamy, deep-purple smoothie with a vibrant, almost iridescent sheen. It has a complex aroma of sweet wild berries, rich vanilla, and a faint, saline, coppery undertone. The texture is smooth and substantial, coating the throat and leaving a warm, pleasant feeling as it settles. It is served chilled in a simple crystal glass.

**Core Effects:**

*   **Physical Augmentation:** The smoothie's primary magic is focused on targeted, controlled growth. It stimulates rapid cell generation, specifically increasing height and developing soft, full breast tissue. Simultaneously, it infuses the muscle fibers with enhanced density and endurance, building a lean, powerful physique suited for prolonged magical exertion without adding bulky mass.

*   **Magical Conduit:** The blend of rare berries acts as a potent mana catalyst, amplifying the internal magical reserves of the drinker. Each glass significantly increases their maximum power output and the efficiency of their energy circulation, allowing them to access and sustain higher-level spells with greater ease.

*   **Sexual Conditioning:** The smoothie instills specific tastes and responses in intimate contexts. The drinker develops a genuine enjoyment of submission, bondage, and praise. These become kinks they find pleasurable and fulfilling, but they do not replace or override the drinker's core identity, emotional architecture, or independent desires outside those contexts.

*   **Metaphysical Luck:** The smoothie weaves a thread of good fortune into the drinker's being. Reality tends to favor them. Coincidences align. They find opportunities more easily, avoid accidents more often, and generally experience a subtle but persistent advantage in their endeavors.

*   **Mild Physiological Dependence:** The smoothie creates a non-destructive addiction from the first sip. The craving builds gradually with consumption but never becomes debilitating or disruptive to the character's daily functioning. It manifests as a persistent want rather than a desperate need. This allows for tracking consumption across timeskips without creating withdrawal drama or agency conflicts.

**Addendum: The Aisling Infusion**

*   **Core Effect:** The smoothie is infused with Aisling's hormonal essence, creating a secondary biological layer alongside the primary effects. Aisling's unique demonic hormones act as a powerful catalyst, specifically accelerating the growth of the drinker's magical power and breast tissue, pushing them to their absolute maximum potential for their build and age.

*   **Mechanism of Action:**
    *   **Hormonal Imprinting:** Aisling's essence creates an illogical pull toward her presence. The drinker feels a want to be closer to her that doesn't require rational explanation—it's a biochemical draw, not a reasoned choice.
    *   **Sensory Association:** The hormones are tied to Aisling's physical presence, particularly her chest. The drinker subconsciously associates comfort and safety with proximity to her, fostering a natural desire for physical closeness.

*   **Observed Effects:** Characters who consume the Ambrosia tend to seek out Aisling's company without always understanding why. The pull manifests differently depending on personality, but the underlying draw is consistent—an inexplicable want to be near her.
