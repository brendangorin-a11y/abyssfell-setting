This sensory overload is managed by a Compartmentalized Focus, a mental discipline that allows her to consciously wall off irrelevant data streams to prevent total cognitive collapse, giving her the appearance of being airheaded when she is, in fact, just prioritizing. Most importantly, she is driven by an Inexorable Deductive Drive, a psychological imperative that compels her to follow any mystery, no matter how dangerous, to its absolute conclusion, treating personal safety as a secondary, often ignored, variable in the grand equation of discovery.
