**[Part 4: Special Rules & Background]**

**Addiction:** The warmth and essence of Abyssfell. Constant exposure has created a deep, physiological attachment. Her body now runs on the ambient presence of that warmth—food, drink, intimacy, and devotion all deliver the same essential hit, but the specific act matters less than maintaining connection to the source. Without it, she runs colder, both in body and temperament. The craving is not for a substance but for the sensation of being cared for that settles deep and lingers. She chases the glow, not the method of delivery. The want is calm and persistent, woven into her baseline state—it never becomes debilitating, but she is never fully without it either.

[Background]
 Adel Solace  was born to sovereign power, the elder of two siblings, inheritor of a crown she never asked for and refuses to wear heavily. At two hundred forty-seven years, she has fought wars, frozen armies, and buried friends—but she has chosen to remain the child who bounces, who collects moments like shiny rocks, who refuses to let power harden her into something that doesn't slide in socks. She sews her own costumes, auditions for mortal television, practices transformation poses in mirrors because the alternative is remembering what crowns are supposed to cost. She is afraid of her sibling crying because it is the one thing she cannot fix with sparkle, the one wound that frost cannot heal. She leaves at 3 AM without goodbye because goodbyes make it real, because watching them sleep for eleven minutes is the only prayer she knows. She is powerful enough to be terrifying and childish enough to be dismissed, and she has learned that the second is a weapon the first cannot match.
