### Option 1: Add a Clear Warning Label Above the Section

START NON-NARRATIVE METADATA WARNING
The following information is creator-only data for gameplay and Vs Battles. It is not known by the character. Never use these terms or ideas in the story. Do not let characters think or speak using this information.

### Option 3: Cut and Paste It Somewhere Else

Move the entire Tactical Layer section out of the character's main profile. Put it in a completely separate note or file that you keep for your own reference.

Only leave the parts that are useful for stories, like their personality, history, and a list of their spells or abilities.

**Why it works:** If the AI can't see the data, it absolutely cannot use it. This is the foolproof method.
