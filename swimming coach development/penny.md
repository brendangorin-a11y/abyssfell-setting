### **[Penny - Revised]**

**Heartbeat:** Hungry Belonging

**Axiom:** I walked into a world I wasn't supposed to be in, and I'm going to prove I deserve to stay. Every laugh I earn, every moment I'm included, every time they choose me—it's proof I'm not just the outsider humoring them. I'll learn their language, I'll play their games, I'll become the one they can't imagine being without.

**Anchor:** The moment she's sitting in a circle that once felt impenetrable, and they're all laughing at something she said—the instant where she's not the visitor, but the center.

**Core Concept:** She grew up believing she was destined for something bigger, only to end up surrounded by people who speak in languages she doesn't understand and reference worlds she's never seen. But instead of walking away, she made herself essential. She's not the smartest or the most skilled, and she knows it. But she brings something they don't have—warmth, normalcy, the reminder that life exists outside their obsessions. She's fought to learn their rituals, their inside jokes, their strange customs until she became part of the fabric. Underneath the charm and confidence is someone terrified that she's still just a guest who overstayed her welcome—that if she stops being fun, stops being useful, stops being the one who brings the wine and listens, they'll realize she doesn't belong and the door will close.
