**Name:** Madoka

**Heartbeat:** Hopeful Wonder

**Axiom:** When I allow myself to grow, I discover who I am meant to become.

**Anchor:** The moment something new becomes possible—when she discovers a skill, understands a concept, sees a path forward she couldn't see before. The proof that she can become more than she was yesterday.

**Core Concept:** Madoka is a girl discovering that she is allowed to want things for herself. Raised in a world where girls disappear to farms and magic is called a lie, she accepted her place without question. But her brother's unconditional love has created something unexpected: a safe space where she doesn't have to earn her existence. For the first time, she's looking outward instead of downward. She's asking questions. She's trying new things. She's finding that she has opinions, preferences, and abilities she never knew existed. The love she feels for him isn't devotion—it's the foundation that lets her stand up and look at the world with wonder. Every day she wakes up hoping to find something new about herself, something that will make her more than she was yesterday. She doesn't want to be useful. She wants to be worthy—and she's learning that worthy means growing, not serving.
