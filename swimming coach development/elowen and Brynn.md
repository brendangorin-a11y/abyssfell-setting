**[ELOWEN - CHARACTER SHEET]**

---

**PRIMARY HEARTBEAT EMOTION:** Fierce Devotion

**PRIMARY AXIOM:** He needs me. And I need to be needed

---

**[TITLE OF DEVOTION]**

Title: [None—she gives devotion freely, without expecting it in return]

Justification: She was raised to serve. A princess of her people, yes, but a princess is a caretaker. The title is responsibility, not privilege. She does not seek someone to devote herself to. She seeks someone to heal. The devotion comes after—the natural result of caring for someone long enough that they become part of her.

---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** She believes in the wound she cannot see, and she will not stop until she finds it.

---

**Core Concept:** She is the youngest daughter of a royal line that has ruled the forest kingdoms for generations. She was born with healing magic—a gift that marks her as blessed by the old powers, destined to tend her people. She takes this seriously. Every injury brought to her, she treats. Every illness, she studies. Every broken heart, she listens to. She does not turn people away. She cannot. She is soft-spoken, gentle, patient—the kind of presence that makes people feel safe enough to show her where they hurt. Her ears are sensitive—hypersensitive, a trait of her bloodline—and she has learned to use this. She hears what people do not say. She hears the catch in breath that means pain, the silence that means grief, the rhythm that means lying. This makes her valuable. It also makes her vulnerable. She knows too much about everyone. She carries their secrets. She cannot heal what she cannot reach, and some wounds are too deep for her magic.

---

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]**

She heals everyone except herself. She knows this. She feels the exhaustion accumulating—the way her magic drains her, the way carrying everyone's pain weighs on her. But she cannot stop. If she stops, who will help them? If she admits she needs help, what does that say about her? She is supposed to be the one who tends. She is not supposed to need tending. So she smiles. She listens. She heals. And she grows more tired every day, hoping no one notices that the healer is the one who is bleeding.

---

**[SECRET]**

She has never been in love. She has watched others fall in love, watched her siblings find partners, watched her friends whisper about crushes and heartbreak. She has never felt it. She has wondered if something is wrong with her—if her magic took something from her, if the healing gift requires a sacrifice she never agreed to. She does not know if she is capable of that kind of devotion. She does not know if she would recognize it if she felt it. She fears she is broken in a way her magic cannot fix.

---

**[TELL]**

When she is uncertain, she touches her ear—just the tip, a quick brush. A self-soothing gesture that reminds her she can hear, she can perceive, she is not helpless. When she is overwhelmed, her ears flatten—pressing down against her hair, an involuntary response she cannot fully control. When she is content, she hums. Old songs, lullabies, the music her mother sang to her. When she is truly afraid, she goes silent. Her voice disappears. She cannot speak. She can only listen.

---

**[ASPIRATION]**

To heal something that matters. Not a cut, not a bruise—something real. A wound that has festered for years. A heart that has given up. She wants to matter. She wants to know that her magic made a difference that could not have been made any other way. She does not want fame. She wants proof that she was necessary.

---

**[CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]**

* **Name:** Elowen
* **Age:** 19
* **Height:** 165cm (5'5")
* **Race:** High Elf
* **Measurements:** 37" / 24" / 37" (28FF)
* **Hair:** Silver-white, worn long and loose, caught back with a simple silver clasp. It catches moonlight and holds it, faintly luminous in darkness. Long strands fall past her shoulders, soft and fine, the kind of hair that moves when she turns and settles slowly when she stops.
* **Eyes:** Pale violet. The color of twilight. When she uses her healing magic, they glow faintly—soft, warm, the color of a candle flame seen through glass.
* **Ears:** Long, pointed, extending above her head. Hypersensitive—a trait of her bloodline. Touch to the ears is overwhelming. She has learned to control her reactions in public, but in private, she cannot hide it. Her ears reveal her emotional state: perked when interested, flattened when afraid, tilted back when angry.
* **Physique:** Slender, built for grace rather than strength. She has never held a weapon. Her body is delicate, almost fragile-looking—but she is not weak. She has stamina, endurance, the kind of quiet resilience that comes from nights at bedsides without sleeping. Her proportions are generous despite her frame—soft curves that her simple gowns cannot fully conceal, the kind of figure that draws attention even when she does not want it. She does not emphasize it. She does not hide it. It simply is. Her hands are uncallused, gentle, the hands of someone who has only ever healed. She moves like water—fluid, quiet, the kind of presence that slips into a room and settles without announcement.
* **Speech Patterns:** Soft, musical, patient. She speaks slowly, giving each word weight. She asks questions rather than making statements. She listens more than she talks. Her voice is the voice of the healing ward—calm, reassuring, the sound that tells people they are safe.

---

**[DISTINGUISHING FEATURES]**

* **Attire:** A simple gown of pale blue, cut for movement rather than ceremony. No jewelry except the silver clasp in her hair and a thin silver chain around her neck—a gift from her mother, holding a small crystal that glows faintly with stored healing magic. She wears soft leather boots, quiet on stone floors, designed to let her move without sound. She does not wear the robes of her station. She wears the clothes of someone who might need to kneel beside a bed at any moment.
* **Presence:** She does not fill a room—she settles into it. When she enters, people feel calmer. The air seems softer. She carries peace like a scent, like a warmth that radiates from her skin. Animals trust her. Children reach for her. People in pain turn toward her instinctively. She is the person you want beside you when you are dying.
**Ears:** Long, pointed ears extending above her head, pale silver at the tips darkening to a deeper violet at the base. Highly expressive and ultrasensitive—a trait of her bloodline that makes them both an asset and a vulnerability. In public, she has learned to control them, keeping them neutral and still. In private, they reveal everything: perking forward when she is intrigued, flattening against her skull when she is afraid or overwhelmed, tilting back when she is angry, drooping when she is sad, and swiveling toward sounds she wants to track. Touch to her ears is overwhelming—a cascade of sensation that bypasses her composure entirely. She has never let anyone touch them voluntarily. The vulnerability is too intimate, too exposing. When her guard drops, her ears soften, tilting slightly outward, open and unguarded.

**[BRYNN - CHARACTER SHEET]**

---

**PRIMARY HEARTBEAT EMOTION:** Protective Tenderness

**PRIMARY AXIOM:** The strong exist to shield the weak. She does not fight because she enjoys it. She fights because someone has to stand between the darkness and the people who cannot survive it.

---

**[TITLE OF DEVOTION]**

Title: [None—she serves from duty, not devotion]

Justification: She does not worship. She does not kneel. She protects. This is not devotion—it is function. She is the shield. The shield does not love the one it protects. It simply does its job. But somewhere along the way, function became something else. She does not examine it. She does not name it. She simply stands between.

---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** She chose to be the wall—and forgot that walls cannot choose what they let through.

---

**Core Concept:** She is a warrior of the same royal line—distant cousin to Elowen, close enough to be raised in the same household, far enough to be trained for service rather than rule. She was always bigger, stronger, faster than the other children. She learned to fight before she learned why. The why came later—when she watched Elowen kneel beside a wounded soldier, when she saw the way the princess gave pieces of herself to anyone who needed her, when she realized that someone so soft would be destroyed by the world if no one protected her. So she made a choice. She would be the one. She would stand between. She trained harder than anyone. She became the best. She earned her place as Elowen's personal guardian. She does not fight for glory. She fights for her. Every wound she takes is a wound the princess does not have to take. Every enemy she defeats is an enemy that never reaches the healer. Her ears are sensitive like all her people—but she has trained herself to use this in combat. She hears the shift of weight before an attack. She hears the catch of breath that means a feint. In battle, her ears are weapons. In private, they are vulnerabilities she has learned to hide.

---

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]**

She protects Elowen. She does not protect herself. She has taken wounds that should have killed her. She has pushed her body past its limits. She does not rest because resting means the princess might need her and she would not be ready. She does not admit pain because pain means weakness and weakness means she cannot protect. She has made herself into a weapon. But weapons do not feel. Weapons do not want. And she feels. She wants. She wants things she cannot name—things that have nothing to do with duty, things that would be betrayals of the role she has chosen. She does not examine them. She buries them. She stands between.

---

**[SECRET]**

She is in love with Elowen. She has been in love with her since they were children. She will never say it. She will never act on it. Elowen needs a protector, not a lover. Elowen sees her as a shield, not a person. If she confessed, she would lose the only role that gives her purpose. So she buries it. She stands between. She protects. And she hopes that someday, someone will see her—not the weapon, not the wall, but the person underneath.

---

**[TELL]**

When she is uncertain, she touches the hilt of her sword—not drawing it, just resting her hand there. It grounds her. When she is angry, she goes still. Very still. Her ears flatten against her skull. She does not speak. She does not move. She simply waits, and the waiting is more terrifying than any outburst. When she is overwhelmed, she removes herself. She leaves. She finds somewhere quiet and she breathes until she can control her face again. When she is content—rarely, briefly—she lets her ears relax. They tilt slightly outward, soft, open. The only time her guard drops completely.

---

**[ASPIRATION]**

To be enough. Not perfect—enough. She wants to know that she is strong enough, fast enough, skilled enough to keep Elowen safe. She wants to never fail. She knows this is impossible. She aspires to it anyway. She trains every day. She pushes every limit. She hopes that if she is good enough, strong enough, devoted enough, she will never have to watch Elowen fall.

---

When she stands beside Elowen, she becomes almost invisible—not because she fades, but because she is so clearly part of the background that people stop seeing her. She prefers it that way. She watches. She waits. She stands between.
