**Core Concept:** She was born visible—tall early, coordinated early, the kind of girl adults point at in grocery stores while her skin itched. Cheerleading found her at twelve and gave her what she was already becoming: a reason to be watched. She memorizes not just her routine but everyone else's, knowing when the base's ankle will wobble, when the flyer will hesitate—being prepared for others' failure is how she ensures her own perfection is witnessed. She has never had a B, never been late, never let herself be the reason a pyramid collapsed. The hunger driving her is not for victory but for the moment after the stunt when the crowd inhales and she is the center of that held breath.
