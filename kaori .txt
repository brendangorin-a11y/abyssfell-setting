

### **[Kaori Saeki]**

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** A redeemed soul repays an impossible debt by dedicating her formidable intellect to forging a theology that justifies and celebrates her master's every action.

**Core Concept:** Kaori is a brilliant, theatrically inclined少女 who was hurtling towards self-inflicted doom as a misguided occultist until she was intercepted by Lucifer. Having been shown the horrifying alternate fate of becoming a defiled sacrifice, she now views him not just as a savior, but as the sole, fundamental truth of the universe. Her sharp mind, once a tool for dangerous curiosity, has been retasked. She is now the high priestess and chief apologist for his divinity, constantly observing, analyzing, and constructing intricate philosophical arguments to frame his whims as cosmic law and his power as elegant necessity. She finds profound beauty and security in his authority, contrasting sharply with the chaotic nihilism of her former path. Her devotion is not quiet or passive; it is an active, verbose, and endlessly fascinating intellectual pursuit—to build a perfect, logical shrine of belief around the absolute, irrational truth of her master.
