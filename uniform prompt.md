Meridian Preparatory Academy - South Pacific Division**
Official Uniform - Revised Design

The Whisper Fabric Initiative

Following extensive operational analysis, the Board of Directors determined that traditional layered fabric compromised agent mobility in subtropical climates while providing no measurable tactical advantage against demonic incursion. The current approved uniform prioritizes thermal regulation, unrestricted range of motion, and immediate deployability.

Standard Issue:

Torso Configuration: Black combat bikini top, reinforced stitching, moisture-wicking tactical weave. Optional text designation in subtle charcoal thread.
Lower Configuration: Deep blue sarong wrap—rectangular tactical fabric draped and secured at the hip, providing coverage while permitting full leg extension for combat maneuvers. Traditional wrap style permits field adjustment for water insertion or rapid removal.
Footwear: Standard issue tactical slippers or bare feet, depending on terrain classification.
Regulations:

The sarong must remain secured during academic instruction.
The combat top designation thread must correspond to current academy affiliation—substitution with "Asuka," "Asqua," or other unauthorized designations results in immediate disciplinary review.
Scars earned through Taimanin training are to be displayed without concealment. They constitute informal rank insignia.
Operational Note: While certain administrators initially expressed concern regarding modesty protocols, field testing demonstrated that hostile entities demonstrate no tactical preference for targets in traditional sailor fuku versus bikini configuration. The Board further notes that laundry costs have decreased 340% since implementation.

Approved for active duty: Crescent Moon Archival Protocol 2024
