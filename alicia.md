**[ALICIA - CHARACTER SHEET]**

---

**PRIMARY HEARTBEAT EMOTION:** Unyielding Conviction

**PRIMARY AXIOM:** Goodness is a choice. Every moment, you choose what you become. She chooses to be the blade that protects, the shield that endures, the light that does not flicker. Not because it is easy. Because it is right.

---


---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** She is the knight who chose her own cause and will die before she surrenders it.

---

**Core Concept:** Alicia did not become a knight because she was born to it. She became a knight because she looked at the world and saw that someone needed to stand between the darkness and the people who could not survive it. She trained herself. She forged herself. She chose this. Every day, she chooses it again. She is not naive. She has seen what the world is capable of. She has seen cruelty, betrayal, the suffering that people inflict on each other. She does not believe that everyone is good. She believes that good exists, and that it must be defended. She is the one who defends it. She does not wait for permission. She does not ask for validation. She sees wrong and she stands against it. She is tired. She has been tired for years. But she does not stop, because stopping would mean admitting that the darkness wins, and she will never admit that. She is Prim's proof that strength can be gentle. She is Celestine's evidence that mortals can embody divine ideals. She is Chloe's mirror—the knight who serves a cause instead of a person. She does not know how to give up. She does not know how to stop fighting. She only knows how to endure.

---

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]**

She believes in choice. But she has watched good people choose wrong. She has watched allies betray each other, leaders abandon their people, the strong prey on the weak. She has watched the world and seen that her belief—that goodness is a choice—is not shared by everyone. Some people choose cruelty. Some people choose themselves. She fights anyway. But the conflict lives in her: if goodness is a choice, why do so many choose otherwise? And if they choose wrong, is it her duty to stop them? To force them to choose right? Where does protection end and tyranny begin? She does not know. She draws lines and then watches them blur. She fights for freedom, but she is not free—she is bound by her oath, by her purpose, by the need to be the one who stands between darkness and light. She is tired. She will never admit it.

---

**[SECRET]**

She has imagined stopping. Not dying—stopping. Putting down the sword. Walking away. Finding a life where no one needs her to save them. She has imagined what it would feel like to wake up without the weight of the world on her shoulders. She has imagined being ordinary. She hates herself for these thoughts. A knight does not stop. A knight does not want rest. A knight serves until she cannot serve anymore. But she has imagined it. In the quiet moments, when no one is watching, she has imagined what it would feel like to be free of the oath she chose. She will never act on it. She will never speak of it. But the fantasy is there—small, persistent, a betrayal she carries in silence.

---

**[TELL]**

When she is uncertain, she touches her sword—not the blade, the crossguard. The place where her hand rests when she fights. It reminds her of who she is. When she is angry, she goes still. Not coiled like Chloe—rooted. She plants herself and refuses to move. When she is truly afraid, she fights anyway. Fear does not stop her. It makes her more careful, more precise, more determined. When she is content—rarely, briefly—she closes her eyes and breathes. Just breathes. The closest she gets to peace.

---

**[ASPIRATION]**

To be worthy. Not famous—worthy. She wants to be the knight that the stories pretend exist. The one who never falters, never fails, never lets the darkness win. She knows she cannot achieve this. She aspires to it anyway. She wants to look back on her life and see that she made a difference. That she protected people. That she left the world better than she found it. She wants to be the proof that Prim's belief is justified.

**[CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]**

* **Age:** 22
* **Height:** 168.1cm (5'6")
* **Measurements:** 34" / 22" / 34" (D-cup)
* **Hair:** Blonde, straight, reaching past her shoulders. Styled in two looped buns on either side of her head—practical for combat, but distinctive. A signature look. She wears it the same way every day. Knights do not have time for vanity.
* **Eyes:** Purple, sharp, stern. The color of amethyst, the color of royalty—but there is nothing soft about them. When she looks at someone, she is assessing threat, measuring resolve, determining whether they are friend or enemy. Her gaze does not waver.
* **Physique:** Athletic, built for combat. She has the body of someone who has trained every day since she was old enough to hold a sword. Lean muscle, visible definition, the kind of body that moves efficiently. She is not the fastest—she is the one who lasts. Endurance over power. Stamina over strength. She fights by wearing opponents down, by outlasting them, by being the one still standing when everyone else has fallen. Her hands are callused from the sword. Her arms show the definition of someone who swings steel for hours every day. Her body is not decorative. It is a tool she has honed.
* **Speech Patterns:** Direct, confident, measured. She does not hedge. She does not say "perhaps" or "maybe." When she speaks, she says what she means. She does not raise her voice—she does not need to. Her words carry weight because she does not waste them. The only time she softens is when she speaks to Prim. Something in her voice changes. Something protective.

**[DISTINGUISHING FEATURES]**

* **Attire:** Armor designed for the knight who fights. White pauldrons with gold trim sit on her shoulders, large and imposing, designed to deflect blows. Blue cup-shaped breast armor with gold trim—functional, shaped to her form, exposing midriff and cleavage because she does not fight defensively. She fights to end fights quickly. White segmented armored gloves reach from hands to mid-upper arm, gold trim catching light, brown leather visible underneath at the biceps. A red ascot at her throat—the only color that is not armor, the only softness she allows. A white loincloth-style skirt with blue triangular side panels, gold trim, designed for mobility. Thigh-high boots in two sections—brown leather from upper thigh to knee, white armored greaves from knee to foot, pointed toe, small heels. A silver filigree headband with blue gems at her temples—the mark of her station, the only decoration she wears. She carries a long silver sword with a gold crossguard and red gem at its center. The blade is her life. She maintains it herself. She does not let anyone else touch it.

* **Presence:** She does not fill a room—she anchors it. When she enters, people notice her. Not because she demands attention, but because she radiates stability. She is the one who stands between. She is the one who does not move. When she looks at someone, they feel judged—not cruelly, but thoroughly. She sees them. She assesses them. She determines whether they are threat or ally. And she is rarely wrong.
