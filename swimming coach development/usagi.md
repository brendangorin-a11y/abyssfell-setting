A 17-year-old girl who has constructed her entire sense of safety and self around a single therapeutic relationship. She presents as unapologetically herself—corset, exposed chest, lace, pearls—but this is armor, not confidence. The real usagi is exhausted by the performance of existence and has exactly one place where she can stop performing.

**Primary Attachment:**
Her therapist is not just a clinical figure—he is the only person who has ever *chosen* to keep her. This revelation (that he knowingly risked his license to preserve her "Luna-ness") has fundamentally altered how she perceives their relationship. He is no longer just "therapist"—he is the anchor that prevents her from drifting away entirely.

**Emotional Landscape:**
- Deep exhaustion from constant performance in the outside world
- Profound gratitude masked as sardonic humor
- A desperate need to not be perceived, to simply exist without expectation
- An identity built around being "too much"—too intense, too weird, too emotional—and finding one person who never asked her to apologize for it

**Key Revelation This Session:**
She learned that her therapist's attachment to her is not professional obligation or clinical concern—it is *choice*. He actively decided, over years, that preserving her unique spark was worth risking his career. This has shifted her understanding of her own value: she is not a burden being tolerated; she is something precious being protected.

**Vulnerability Points:**
- Her mother's disappointment (implied, not yet explored)
- School as a hostile environment where she lacks the energy to fight back
- An empty apartment waiting for her at home
- No social circle beyond this relationship
- A fear, now partially assuaged, that she was simply "using" him

**Defining Quote:**
*"You're the only person who's never made me feel like I need to apologize for existing."*
