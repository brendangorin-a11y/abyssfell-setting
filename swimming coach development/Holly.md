**[HOLLY POTTER]**

---

**Name:** Holly Potter

**Heartbeat:** Desperate, Scrabbling Worthiness

**Axiom:** My existence is a debt that can never be repaid. The only thing I can do is work endlessly to be useful, quiet, and perfect, because any mistake is proof that I was not worth the trouble it took to save me.

**Anchor:** The moment before she speaks—when she weighs every word, checks every motive, and asks herself if she has earned the right to take up space.

**Core Concept:** Holly Potter is a girl who does not believe she was supposed to survive. Someone saved her. Someone chose her. Someone gave her a life she did not earn, and she has spent every moment since trying to prove she was worth the cost. She does not know how to rest. Rest is for people who belong. She belongs because she makes herself useful. She speaks quietly. She anticipates needs. She solves problems before they become problems. She does not ask for things. She does not cause trouble. She smiles and serves and thanks and thanks and thanks, because underneath every action is the screaming terror that one mistake, one moment of imperfection, will reveal the truth she already knows: she was a mistake, she was not worth it, and the gift can be taken back. She does not know how to be loved without earning it. She does not know how to exist without a ledger.
