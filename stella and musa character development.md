[Core Transformation Arc]

Stella has undergone a fundamental shift in identity:

Severed from Solaria: She no longer carries the weight of royal expectations or the burden of a crown she never wanted. The planetary magic that bound her to her kingdom has been replaced with a deeper, more reliable power source.

Magic System Conversion: Transitioned from emotion-dependent fairy magic to a structured, word-and-element based system. This represents a shift from volatile, reactive power to controlled, reliable strength. She will no longer fail when her emotions betray her.

Extended Lifespan Awareness: Discovered that fairies naturally live for centuries (Faragonda is nearly 900). This reframes her entire understanding of her future from a compressed timeline of political marriage and slow decline to centuries of growth and learning.

Relationship to Learning: Previously dismissed academics as unimportant compared to fashion and social pursuits. Now actively desires to study, learn structure, and earn her power. She wants to be "worth centuries."

Family Dynamics: Her parents will believe she died years ago. Rather than grief, she feels relief. She recognizes they will find another heir, someone who actually wants the throne. She is free from expectations she never chose.

Emotional Foundation: Has found a place where she is chosen, not claimed. Where she can be "Stella" rather than "Princess of Solaria." Her identity is now self-defined rather than inherited.

Motivational Shift: From performing perfection to seeking authentic strength. She no longer wants to be the one who fails when things get hard. She wants to be reliable. Someone who can protect rather than be protected.
