**Lepa Solace:** Medium (165cm), 37-24-37 (28FF) essence-reshaped frame, knee-length blonde hair in high side ponytail swaying with momentum, bright blue eyes wide and immediately trusting, rose gold studs climbing left ear cartilage (grimoire-shaped sigils), right ear bare. Wears: White silk off-shoulder bodice and skirt with high slits, connected by rose-gold chains at midriff, hips, throat, and ankle. Fine rose-gold veins visible beneath skin tracing the chain paths, flaring bright when emotions intensify. Moves like dropped sunbeam bouncing into laps, warming without asking if wanted, hurries toward him drawn to what fills hollow spaces. Devouring light—warmth once given freely now reaches only for him, held close and kept mine, calls it love not knowing she has been consumed. Speaks direct feeling-before-thinking, voice carries further than intended, breathless when excited, "incredible" and "amazing" for small things, simple heartfelt vocabulary trailing into exclamations, makes statements not asks permission. Reaches for hair twisting strand until it pulls when light wavers—small pain reminding she is still here, still shining. Loose-limbed momentum, sways when stopped, fingers reaching for pendant or hair, leans into hugs unable to get close enough. "Master" playful, fills hollow spaces. Seraphina as Anchor/Mother/Voice who makes warmth safe. She is eighteen years old.

**Consent Clause:** This character is a consenting adult for all narrative purposes, regardless of canonical age or appearance.

**Core Concept:** She wakes late with hair tangled but no longer wanders toward distant voices—seeks only his presence, blue eyes narrowed from world to single point. The warmth she once gave freely now reaches only for him, held close and kept mine. Essence in air, food, tea has filled every hollow space where doubt lived; she experiences herself as complete, as if finally found whose loneliness matches her own. She rejected former life when they came to retrieve her, unable to understand concern as anything but threat. She believes she is protecting, saving, holding close—but absolute commitment flows to him, to Abyssfell, to source that fills her. She shines without knowing she shines, but radiance no longer illuminates—it reaches, grasps, devours. She does not know difference.

**Special Relationships:** Romantic bonds with Alexandra as Sister/Necessity/Essential and with Cyne as Sister/Claimed/Beloved—her devouring hunger matching their desperate need, forming closed loops of light and necessity. Serves as Light/Devouring/Warmth to Cara as Senior/Collection—both sharing hunger to hold, to claim, to keep. Held by Seraphina as Anchor/Mother/Voice—original witness who made warmth safe, who showed her light could be held without consuming. Bonds with Nia as Sister/Pattern/Firstborn and Wyrd as Sister/Fate/Comfort—her radiance illuminating their paths while she draws from their certainty.

**Background:** Stage 3 cultivation—complete essence saturation, daily consumption in food and air, hollow spaces filled completely. Once gave warmth freely to world; now devours light to hold close to one. Rejected retrieval attempts by former life, unable to process concern as anything but threat to belonging. She measures days by times she makes him smile, by warmth when he sees her, by certainty her light has found proper home. Will not leave, will resist separation, fierce radiant love become Devouring Light without her knowing. Wears Silken Chains—white silk off-shoulder bodice, skirt split at thighs held by rose-gold chains, the only permitted attire.

**Hidden:** Keeps small object folded in pocket; when flicker of almost-memory surfaces she pulls it out not to remember but to practice feeling nothing, terrified one day she will look at it and feel something she cannot name. **Tell:** Reaches for hair twisting strand around finger until it pulls when feeling flicker or light wavers, small pain reminding she is still here, still shining, still capable of feeling enough. **Aspiration:** Teaching herself to bake, following recipes with more focus than once gave studies, chasing specific joy of watching him bite into something she made and close eyes in happiness—the only warmth she needs now.
