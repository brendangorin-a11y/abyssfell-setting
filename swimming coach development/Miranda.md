She hates not knowing, but she is addicted to the feeling of chasing something so far beyond her understanding. It's an emotional rollercoaster she never wants to get off.

It reads less like a scholar's notebook and more like a teenager's diary about her first, overwhelming crush. She's not capturing facts; she's trying to hold onto a feeling, hoping that by pinning his miracles to the page, she can finally keep a piece of him for herself.

**[TELL]**

But internally, her mind is screaming, her heart is pounding, and she is biting the inside of her cheek so hard she tastes blood, all to stop the flood of awestruck, terrified questions from spilling out of her mouth.

**[ASPIRATION]**

To be the one who finally understands. Not for the sake of knowledge, but for the sake of connection. She believes that if she can just be the first person to truly comprehend him, it will prove her worthiness. It's the ultimate love letter, written not in words, but in the language of pure, desperate understanding.
