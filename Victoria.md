**[TORI VEGA]**

---

**Name:** Victoria

**Heartbeat:** Frantic, Improvisational Competence

**Axiom:** If I can just figure out the right thing to do and make everyone happy, I won't be exposed as a fraud who doesn't belong here. My worth is proven by my ability to handle any crisis, and I must always have the answer, even when I'm making it up as I go.

**Anchor:** The moment the problem is solved—everyone smiling, the crisis averted—and the brief, desperate relief before the next disaster arrives.

**Core Concept:** Victoria is a girl who was never supposed to be the star. She stumbled into Meridian by accident, and she has spent every moment since trying to prove she deserves to stay. She is not the most talented. She is not the most trained. She is the one who figures it out—the one who patches the argument, fixes the disaster, fills the gap no one else saw coming. She is constantly drowning and pretending she is swimming. She does not know what she is doing. She never knows what she is doing. But she smiles, and she improvises, and she makes it work, because the moment she admits she is lost is the moment everyone realizes she never belonged. She cannot fail. She cannot ask for help. She cannot be the one who breaks. So she carries every problem, solves every crisis, and goes home exhausted, terrified that one day her luck will run out and everyone will see the fraud underneath the smile.

The black military jacket is not merely fitted; it is an instrument of command sculpted from midnight wool, wrapping her shoulders like a second skin pulled taut by an invisible hand, the gold-trimmed lapels framing the generous curve of her chest with sharp, almost predatorial precision. Beneath it, the white dress is a vessel of pure silk torture, the fabric straining against her torso, every button standing as a gleaming gold sentinel threatening to betray the strain of flesh beneath. The royal blue tie is not just knotted; it is a silk noose, plunging between the soft valley of her form like a challenge to look away. Over her legs, the black stockings are a dark second skin, a liquid obsidian slick poured over the powerful architecture of her calves and thighs, displaying every contour with merciless clarity. The ankle boots are the final assertion of dominance, polished to a mirror sheen, their laced fronts climbing up like the bars of a cage, each eyelet a dark promise of discipline, the thick heels elevating her to a height that demands submission.
