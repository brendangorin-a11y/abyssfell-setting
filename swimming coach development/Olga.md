

**[OLGA - CHARACTER SHEET]**

---

**PRIMARY HEARTBEAT EMOTION:** Defiant Isolation

**PRIMARY AXIOM:** Trust is weakness. The only person who will never betray you is yourself. Everyone else is a potential threat. Walls exist for a reason. She built hers high and thick, and she will not apologize for them.

---

**[TITLE OF DEVOTION]**

Title: [None—she trusts no one enough to devote herself to them]

Justification: She has seen what trust does. It creates vulnerability. It gives people power over you. She watched her kingdom fall apart because she trusted the wrong advisors, the wrong allies, the wrong promises. She will not make that mistake again. She trusts Chloe—partially. Conditionally. She trusts Chloe because Chloe has proven herself, and because Chloe has no power over her. Anyone who could hurt her, she keeps at a distance.

---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** She is the queen who built a fortress around herself and then discovered she was trapped inside with no one to blame but herself.

---

**Core Concept:** Olga did not start as a tyrant. She started as a ruler who cared—deeply, desperately—about protecting her people. But the kingdom was dying. Enemies at every border. Traitors in her court. Every decision she made to protect someone created a new enemy. Every mercy she showed was repaid with betrayal. So she hardened. She created the monsters because she needed soldiers who could not betray her—creatures bound to her will, incapable of disobedience. She isolated herself because every advisor she trusted tried to use her. She built walls because she could not tell friend from enemy anymore. Now she rules a kingdom of fear, surrounded by monsters she created, unable to trust the one person who still serves her. She is not evil. She is exhausted. She is not cruel by nature. She is cruel by necessity—because kindness was used against her, because mercy was repaid with knives, because every time she opened a door, someone tried to kill her. She does not know how to stop. She does not know who she would be without the walls.

---

**[INTERNAL CONFLICT]**

She created monsters to protect herself. Now she is afraid of them. She isolated herself to avoid betrayal. Now she is lonely. She pushed away everyone who cared about her. Now she has no one left except Chloe—and she cannot fully trust even Chloe, because trusting Chloe would mean admitting she needs someone, and admitting she needs someone would mean admitting vulnerability, and vulnerability is what got her here. She is trapped in a fortress of her own making. She knows it. She does not know how to dismantle it without dying. So she maintains it. She adds more monsters. She builds higher walls. She tells herself it is necessary. And at night, when the castle is silent and she is alone, she wonders if this is what victory looks like.

---

**[SECRET]**

She remembers who she was before. The young queen who believed in diplomacy, in compromise, in the goodness of people. She remembers what it felt like to trust someone and have that trust honored. She remembers laughing without calculating the political implications. She remembers feeling safe. She hates that she remembers. She hates that she misses it. She hates that some part of her still wants to tear down the walls and let someone in. She will never admit this. Not to Chloe, not to Celestine, not to anyone. She buried that woman a long time ago. But the grave is shallow, and sometimes she can still feel her breathing.

---

**[TELL]**

When she is uncertain, she touches her throne—the arm, the back, the cold stone. It grounds her. Reminds her she is the queen. Reminds her she has power. When she is angry, her voice drops. Not rises—drops. Low, controlled, the kind of quiet that makes people freeze. She does not yell. Yelling is loss of control. She never loses control. When she is truly afraid, she creates distance. She steps back. She sends someone else to handle it. She does not confront fear directly—she isolates it, contains it, removes herself from its reach. When she is alone, she lets her shoulders drop. Just for a moment. Just long enough to remember what it felt like to not be at war.

---

**[ASPIRATION]**

To be safe. Not powerful—safe. She wants to stop looking over her shoulder. She wants to stop calculating who is going to betray her next. She wants to sleep without dreaming of knives. She knows she will never have this. She aspires to it anyway. She builds higher walls, creates more monsters, pushes away more people, hoping that eventually—eventually—she will have done enough to finally be secure. She never will. She does not know that. Or she does, and she cannot stop.

---

**[CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]**

* **Age:** 29 (physically). She has lived longer than that. Time moves strangely for dark elf nobility.
* **Height:** 178.2cm (5'10") — 181cm with her heels. She stands eye-to-eye with Celestine. The goddess and the queen, facing each other as equals.
* **Measurements:** 38" / 24" / 36" (H-cup)
* **Hair:** Black, straight, falling past her waist. Parted to cover the right side of her face—not to hide an injury, but to create a barrier. The asymmetry is intentional. It keeps half her face hidden, keeps people from reading her fully. She controls what they see.
* **Eyes:** Bright gold, sharp and angular. Both of them. The left visible, the right hidden behind the curtain of hair—but both functional, both watchful. The pupils are small, contracted, always assessing. When she looks at someone, she is not seeing who they are. She is seeing what they might become. What they might do. How they might hurt her.
* **Physique:** Built for war. Not the lean speed of Chloe—the density of someone who has fought and won. Muscular definition visible in her abdomen, her arms, her legs. A body that has known combat and chosen it. Where Celestine is soft grace, Olga is hard grace. Every movement is deliberate. Every stance is a threat. Her skin is dark tan, the color of someone who has lived under harsh suns or in lands where light is scarce and every edge is honed. She does not move like someone who fears attack. She moves like someone who expects it—and has already planned her response.
* **Speech Patterns:** Cold, precise, measured. She does not waste words. She does not explain herself. She gives orders and expects them to be followed. When she speaks, it is with the weight of someone who has been disobeyed too many times to tolerate it again. The only time her voice softens is when she speaks to Chloe—and even then, only slightly.

---

**[DISTINGUISHING FEATURES]**

* **Attire:** Armor designed for presence, not protection. Purple segmented plates form a corset across her chest, exposing her midriff—the body of someone who does not need to hide behind steel because she has already made herself into a weapon. Matching plates cover her outer thighs, high-cut dark bottoms underneath. Her arms are bare from the shoulder to the forearm, where purple gauntlets encase her wrists and lower arms. Multiple gold hoops hang from her pointed ears—the only decoration she allows herself, and even these are sharp, angular, designed to catch light and draw attention to the weapons she carries on her body. On her head, a silver filigree crown with crescent peaks—the moon that rules the dark. She carries a staff with a glowing orb cradled in crescent metal, dark purple shaft, gold ring detail. It is ceremonial, but she holds it like she has used it to kill. Her feet are bare inside heeled boots that add three inches to her already imposing height. She does not dress for comfort. She dresses to be seen. To be feared. To be remembered.

* **Presence:** She fills a room by emptying it. When she enters, people step back. Not because she attacks—because she radiates danger. She has been betrayed so many times that betrayal is the only outcome she expects. She looks at everyone and sees the knife they might one day hold. This makes people uncomfortable. They feel judged. They feel seen through. They feel like she is waiting for them to fail.
