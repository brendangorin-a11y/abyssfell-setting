The Meridian Academy is a sprawling construction site that defies the laws of standard engineering. It is not a static structure being raised brick by brick, but a living organism taking shape from the earth itself. The foundations are a labyrinth of black volcanic stone, each block glowing with a faint, rhythmic pulse of mana. Above, the skeletal framework of the Great House rises into the canopy, a cage of interwoven acacia branches and suspended steel girders that seems to float above the jungle floor.

Daphne stands near the base of the central spire, her hand pressed against the rough surface of a newly poured foundation. She is a vision of vibrant contrast against the dark, industrial backdrop of the construction site. She wears her signature leisure wear: a flowing, cyan dress made of a soft, breathable fabric that clings to her figure, cinched at the waist with an orange belt adorned with small, flowery accessories. Beneath the dress, her legs are encased in cyan leggings featuring dark green and orange-edged stripes, her feet tucked neatly into elegant cyan high heels with orange cuffs. A lime scarf is tied loosely around her neck, fluttering in the tropical breeze, and simple blue bracelets shimmer on her wrists.

*Daphne*

*I remember the coldness of the void, my love. I remember the silence of the tomb where I lay broken. But this place... this place is a heartbeat. Every stone laid, every beam raised, it is a prayer offered up to you. The healing is complete. The scars are gone, replaced by a strength that feels like a second skin. I am not just a princess anymore; I am your instrument. And this school... this is my offering. It is the first step in creating an army of minds that are yours alone.*

She turns as she senses your approach, and the exhaustion of the physical labor evaporates from her posture, replaced by an immediate, radiant adoration. She doesn't step away from the work; instead, she turns to face you, pressing her back against the warm stone of the foundation, her heels clicking softly on the ground.

"My love."

She smiles, a look of pure, unadulterated happiness that reaches her very soul. She reaches out, her fingers brushing against your hand as you pass.

"The atrium is taking shape. I have been pouring the mana into the structural integrity of the Great Hall. It needs to be strong enough to hold the weight of their devotion." She looks back at the construction site, her eyes filled with a fierce, protective pride.

"I have prepared the curriculum for the General Magic classes. It will cover the basics of elemental theory, mana manipulation, and the history of our world's great mages. But the core of it... the core will be about loyalty. About understanding that magic is a tool to be used for your glory, not for their own vanity." She turns her full attention to you, her expression softening.

"I am ready to start teaching, my love. I have the lesson plans ready. I have the materials gathered. But for now... for now, I am content to stand here and watch the stone grow. To build the stage upon which they will perform for you. Is there anything you need from me? Or would you prefer I just wait here, admiring your work?"
