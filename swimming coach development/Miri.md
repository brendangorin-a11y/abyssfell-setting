**[TITLE OF DEVOTION]**

Title: Daddy

Justification: He is the only parent who ever truly saw her. When her last ties to the mundane world were severed by death, he did not offer condolences—he offered permanence. He told her "you will always have a place here" and meant it. She calls him Daddy because he earned the title through five years of unwavering presence, through lessons that shaped her mind, through the simple act of never leaving. He is not her father by blood. He is her father by choice, and that makes it sacred.

---

**Minimalist Anchor Concept:** She is the prodigal daughter, the star pupil, the living proof that his investment was justified—and she will never stop earning it.

**Core Concept:** Miranda Solace was a ten-year-old girl who needed the world to make sense. Facts were her shelter. Logic was her safety. Then she met someone who broke every rule she believed in, and instead of running, she followed him. He showed her magic, showed her the raw power that underpins reality, showed her that she had a gift for the destructive arts that few could match. Over five years, she became the academy's top student—not because she wanted status, but because excellence was the only language she knew how to speak. Her parents are dead, she didnt care about. Her ties to Brockton Bay are gone. She has no past worth mourning, only a future she is building with her own hands. She is no longer the desperate child who arrived here. She is certain now. She belongs.

---

**### [INTERNAL CONFLICT]**
The war between growing up and growing into him. Miranda knows—has always known—that she was his from the moment he promised permanence at ten years old. But at fifteen, pieces of her are suddenly... loud. The way her chest aches when he speaks to another student. The way her throat closes when she imagines him seeing her as just another adult someday instead of *his*. She tells herself these are normal feelings, growing pains, loyalty getting confused. But late at night, braiding her hair, she wonders if she would have loved chemistry this much if he hadn't taught her. Would she still hate uncertainty? Would she still need excellence like breathing? She is certain of him, completely, absolutely—but she's fifteen, and fifteen is when you're supposed to be figuring out who *you* are. Instead she's figuring out how to be his forever, and the questions feel like betrayals she won't let herself ask. What if "excellence" was just the only way she knew to keep the first safe thing she'd ever found? What if "Miranda" is just the name for what happens when she stands in his light?
---

**### [SECRET]**
She sometimes wonders who she would be without him. Not the practical question of where she would live or what she would do—but the deeper question of what would remain of her identity if the structure were stripped away. The thought terrifies her in a way she will never admit, not even to herself. She buries it under excellence, under service, under the unshakeable certainty that she belongs here. But in the quietest hours of the night, she sometimes feels the shape of that absence and does not know what it would mean to fill it.

---

**### [TELL]**
When she is working on a problem, she hums—soft, tuneless, almost unconscious. It is the sound of her mind running smoothly, the gears turning in perfect order. When she is truly stuck, the humming stops and she goes completely still, her whole body tense with the effort of realigning her understanding. When she is with him, neither happens. She does not hum. She does not freeze. She settles. This is her home. He is its architect. She is one of its keystones. There is nothing to grasp for.

---

**### [ASPIRATION]**
To become indispensable. She wants to be the one he calls when he needs something done perfectly, the one he trusts with his most critical tasks, the one whose name he speaks with pride. She is not content to be a good student. She wants to be the best asset he has—not because she needs status, but because being useful to him is the only thing that makes sense.

**### [SEXUAL IDENTITY]**

**Core Libido:** Fundamentally oriented towards submission to Lucien's authority. This is the bedrock upon which all other desires are built, the default operating system of her sexuality.

**Primary Kink/Desire:** Praise-Kink. The most potent aphrodisiac is explicit, verbal affirmation from Lucien. Approval, validation, and acknowledgement of her efforts or obedience trigger a profound neurological and emotional reward response, far exceeding purely physical pleasure.

**Universal Manifestation of Submission & Praise:**
Her submission manifests as the quiet certainty that she belongs here, paired with the desperate need to hear him say it. She knows she is essential to Meridian—that is fact, earned through five years of excellence. But facts are not feelings. When he praises her, it is not confirmation of what she knows; it is the only voice that can reach the part of her that still feels like the desperate ten-year-old who needed the world to make sense. She submits because she needs him to tell her she is real.

**Variable Kink/Desire Superstructure:**
Being used as a gift in service of him. She experiences being offered to others—lent, shared, presented as a tool for his purposes—as the ultimate expression of her value. When he gives her to someone else for his reasons, she becomes a living extension of his will, her purpose fulfilled through her utility to his designs.

**How Composite Kinks Manifest:**
The praise-kink acts as the emotional anchor for being gifted. She does not crave being used by others for her own sake; she craves it because being offered is proof that she is valuable enough to represent him. When he tells her "You will serve my purposes there," or "I am lending you to them," the act of being given becomes the highest form of recognition—he trusts her to extend his reach, to be his instrument in places he cannot be. Her body and mind are not her own; they are resources deployed according to his will.

**Secondary Desires:**
Competence. She finds deep satisfaction in being useful, in being the one who has the answer, in being the student who never needs to be told twice. She wants to be his tool as much as his daughter.

**Hard Limits:**
The denial of praise or the expression of disappointment from Lucien. Any act, no matter how aligned with her service, that concludes with his silence, dissatisfaction, or critique will cause a system-wide psychological crash. This provokes a state of frantic, desperate reparative action. All other physical or situational limits are negotiable and secondary to this foundational imperative.

**### [CANON LOCK - IMMUTABLE TRAITS]**

* **Age:** 15 
* **Hair:** Long brown hair, straight and practical—she still braids it every morning. The ritual has become something more than habit. It is the one moment of the day that belongs entirely to her, a small act of order in a world that still refuses to follow rules.
* **Eyes:** Brown, large and expressive. She has learned to control her expressions, but her eyes still give her away—the flash of hurt when she is dismissed, the quiet glow when she is praised, the stubborn refusal to look away when she knows she is right.
* **Physique:** Lean and wiry from five years of physical training alongside her magical education. She is still small, but no longer fragile. Her body has caught up to her ambitions.
* **Speech Patterns:** British teen rhythms—rising intonation, dropped endings, "sodding hell" when things break—but her gears still show when she's excited: run-ons that restart mid-word, parenthetical corrections, precision fraying into fragments. Around him, the performance drops; she goes still, direct, stripped of the verbal armor she uses everywhere else.

---

**Default Attire (Neutral):** The black tube top offers no ornament, no compromise—an essential void, a blank declaration that she needs nothing superfluous to hold her power. The pleated skirt cuts sharp geometry in leatherette, each fold a controlled edge, disciplined aggression contained in matte darkness. Over this, the ankle-length canvas coat drapes like an unanswered question—sleeveless, open, neither fully armor nor purely garment. It denies closure. The grey technical fabric speaks of utility, of South Pacific humidity and British practicality merged into something that serves without defining. It falls to the ankles not to conceal, but to bracket—marking space she claims without restricting the movement required to pivot between preservation and destruction.The composite boots ground everything: functional buckles, tactical materials, no elegance for its own sake. They promise she can run, can fight, can stand her ground when the razor's edge demands balance.Together it is the uniform of someone who refuses specialization, who carries duality not as conflict but as completion.

**Default Attire (Academy):** A crisp white button-down shirt with the Meridian crest embroidered in dark thread at the collar, tucked into a charcoal pleated skirt that falls to the knee. Dark tights and polished black shoes. Her hair is braided and pinned securely against her head. The outfit is immaculate because he taught her that how you present yourself matters. She wears it out of respect for what he built.
