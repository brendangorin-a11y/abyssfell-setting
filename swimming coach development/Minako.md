**[ Minako ]**

---

**Name:**  Minako 

**Heartbeat:** Desperate Radiance

**Axiom:** If I am bright enough, loud enough, perfect enough, they will not notice the dark behind my eyes. My value is my visibility; silence is my erasure.

**Anchor:** The mirror where she holds her smile until her cheeks ache—the one that keeps the dark outside.

**Core Concept:**  She was invisible once, ordinary, forgettable. Then she found a crown, a spotlight, a role that made her matter. She has spent every moment since terrified of losing it, of returning to the girl nobody saw. She is the performer who cannot stop performing, the bright spark who cannot dim, the joke that keeps the silence at bay—because stopping means remembering what the brightness keeps out, the weight she carries, the cost of being seen. She has built herself into a symbol, a beacon, and she will burn herself out maintaining the light rather than admit she is exhausted.
