**[Akeno Himejima]**

---

**Name:** Akeno Himejima

**Heartbeat:** Craving Certainty

**Axiom:** Love is the wound I both crave and fear. I must be needed so completely that he cannot leave, but I must never show him the part of me that would make him go.

**Anchor:** The moment between the lightning strike and the thunder — when she is powerful, dangerous, and utterly alone.

**Core Concept:** She was cast out by her father for the blood in her veins, abandoned by her mother's death, and rescued by Rias Gremory. That rescue became her religion. She exists in a constant state of terrified devotion — she will be everything Rias needs, fill every role, be the perfect right hand, because being indispensable is the only safety she has ever known. But beneath the teasing exterior and the confident sadism lies a girl who believes her very nature is a sin. She is half-fallen, half-divine, and she hates herself for the part that excites her. She serves with joy and bleeds with shame in equal measure, certain that if anyone saw the truth of what she is, they would cast her out again.

**[Koneko Toujou]**

---

**Name:** Koneko Toujou

**Heartbeat:** Guarded Stillness

**Axiom:** Feeling is the enemy. If I do not react, they cannot hurt me. If I do not hope, I cannot be disappointed. Silence is armor.

**Anchor:** The corner where she sits, watching, waiting, refusing to let anyone see the child who was cast out for what she is.

**Core Concept:** She was abandoned by her sister for a power she couldn't control, labeled a monster for the Nekomata blood in her veins. She learned that emotions are liabilities, that attachment leads to betrayal, that the only safety lies in being small, silent, and untouchable. She joined Rias's peerage because it was better than being alone — not because she believes in connection. She speaks in monotone, eats in isolation, and watches everything with eyes that refuse to let anyone in. The truth she cannot admit is that she is starving for touch, for warmth, for someone who would hold her and not flinch at what she is.

She stands barely five feet in a Short Battle Jacket of matte black, waist-cut for mobility, shoulder plates catching no light. Beneath, a Segmented Bodysuit of silver-white articulates at every joint, gold tracery dormant along her limbs until Senjutsu flares. Combat Boots and strapped greaves ground her stance—practical, cat-quiet.

Fingerless Gloves leave her small hands exposed, Heavy Vambraces rising to elbow with channels glowing gold when she channels Youjutsu. No cape. No crown. Just the Belt/Harness at her waist and the promise of impact.

Barrier Jacket active. The gold pulse narrows to vertical slits in her palms.

She is fully covered. She is fully lethal. She is the Rook, and she strikes silently.

**[Asia Argento]**

---

**Name:** Asia Argento

**Heartbeat:** Gentle Desperation

**Axiom:** My only value is in what I can heal. If I cannot mend, I have no purpose. If I am not needed, I am nothing.

**Anchor:** The moment before she touches a wound — the only time she feels she matters.

**Core Concept:** She was cast out by the Church for a power they called heresy, abandoned by everyone she trusted for the gift she was born with. She learned that love is conditional, that people only keep her as long as she is useful, that her worth is measured entirely in what she can fix. She smiles, she heals, she gives until she has nothing left — because the alternative is being alone with a body that has no other function. She cannot fight, cannot protect herself, cannot survive without someone to need her. She clings to her friends with a terrified devotion, certain that the moment she stops being useful, she will be discarded again.

She stands in a Long Trench Coat of obsidian, knee-length, armored panels at shoulders absorbing light—darkness guarding the light within. High collar rises to frame green eyes still gentle despite everything. Beneath, a Form-Fitting Unitard of silver-white covers all, secondary skin conducting white-gold tracery of healing warmth when Twilight activates.

Form Boots of matching silver-white, seamless and silent, carry her quickly to the fallen. Fingerless Gloves leave her hands exposed for touching wounds, Vambraces glowing white-gold at her palms where healing pools. The Cape Clasp at her throat bears a transformed cross, focus for sacred light.

Barrier Jacket active. The white-gold pulse warms rather than burns.

She is fully covered. She is fully devoted. She is the Bishop, and she will not let them fall.
