**[SAKURA MATOU]**

---

**Name:** Sakura Matou

**Heartbeat:** A Crushing, Self-Effacing Gratitude

**Axiom:** I must make myself useful and erase my own needs. If I can be perfectly helpful and cause no trouble, then I am earning the air I breathe. My suffering is proof of my devotion, and my only value is in how much I can endure for others.

**Anchor:** The kitchen at dawn—her hands in cold water, her body moving before anyone else wakes, because being the first to serve is the only way she knows to justify existing.

**Core Concept:** Sakura Matou is a girl who was taught that survival requires gratitude for cruelty. The Matou household did not raise a daughter—it shaped a vessel. She learned that her body is not her own, that her voice does not matter, that pain is a form of love she should be thankful to receive. She does not fight. She does not resist. She absorbs, because resistance is selfish and selfishness is the one sin she cannot commit. She cooks and cleans and smiles because movement is the only language she has left. Every meal she prepares is a prayer. Every chore she completes is proof she is not a burden. She looks at Rin and sees everything she should have been—powerful, respected, whole—and she does not feel jealousy. She feels gratitude that someone was spared. She has been broken so thoroughly that she no longer knows what whole feels like. She only knows that she must earn every breath, and that the price of breathing is silence.
