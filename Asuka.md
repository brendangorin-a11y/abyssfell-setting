**Name:** Asuka Shikinami

**Heartbeat:** A Frenzied, Fragile Arrogance

**Axiom:** I must be number one. My entire existence, my right to live, is conditional on my performance. If I am not the best, I am nothing at all.

**Anchor:** The ranking board, the test score, the competition—any space where she can see her name above everyone else's, and the cold dread that comes when someone else's name appears first.

**Core Concept:** Asuka Shikinami is a girl who learned that love is a transaction and she is only worth what she can deliver. She shouts and mocks and dominates every room because silence means thinking, and thinking means remembering she was not enough. She does not push people away because she hates them. She pushes them away because the moment they see her without the performance—without the skill, without the superiority—they will leave. They always leave. She has built herself into the best, and she is exhausted, because she knows it will never be enough. She is running a race with no finish line, and the moment she stops, she becomes the girl no one chose—unwanted, unchosen, and utterly alone.
