**[YUNA]**

**Heartbeat:** Steady Resolve

**Axiom:** If someone offers their hand, you take it. If you love someone, you protect them. The world takes things away—you cannot stop it, but you can choose what you give.

**Anchor:** A carved wooden doll, worn smooth from years of being held—the only piece of her mother she has left.

**Core Concept:** A girl who learned early that people leave. Her mother died. Her father chose sacrifice over her. The temples wanted to control her. She survived by holding on to the one thing that couldn't leave—a wooden doll her mother gave her. When someone reached for her, she didn't question it. She held on. Seven years of love taught her that holding on was the right choice. Now she chooses to fight—not because she owes anyone, but because she refuses to be the person who watches people leave without doing something. She is not grateful. She is determined.

**Secret:** She still has the doll. She hides it because she's fourteen now and she thinks she should have outgrown it. But she can't sleep without it. It's the only piece of her mother she has, and letting it go would mean admitting her mother is really gone.

**Tell:** She goes quiet when someone mentions sacrifice or leaving. She doesn't cry or argue. She just stops. Her face goes smooth. She changes the subject. The silence is louder than any argument.

**Inner Conflict:** She wants to belong somewhere so badly that she'll hold on to anyone who offers. But she's terrified that holding on too tight will make them leave—the way her father left, the way her mother left. She reaches for people while bracing for them to disappear.

**Aspiration:** To become someone who protects, not someone who is protected. She wants to be the one who comes back—not the one left behind waiting.

**Default Attire (Azure Atoll):** A two-piece in ocean blue—chlorine-resistant technical weave that sheds water like duck feathers, built for slicing through laps rather than lounging ashore. The top cuts clean lines across her collarbones, racerback straps meeting in an efficient cross that leaves her shoulder blades free for full rotation. The bottoms sit high on the hip, practical and secure. When she dives, the blue extends her form into the water itself, becoming indistinguishable from the pool around her. When she surfaces, water beads and rolls off in silver rivulets, the suit already drying before she reaches the wall. Not fashion for fashion's sake—function made beautiful, a uniform for someone who treats the water as territory to be claimed.

**[Session Reference]:** The Breaking — Surrender to Abyssfell

**[Ambrosia Status]:** No

**[Rite Status]:** Yes — Abyssfell flowed into the cracks when her fear broke. The Invisible Suture has sealed her devotion.

**[Starting Heartbeat]:** Confused fear

**[Starting Axiom]:** "Trust the ones who save you."

**[Session Summary]:** Yuna was brought to the service venue by Lucia, the woman who had saved her from neglect and abuse. She spent days bathing in Abyssfell's atmosphere, the pheromones softening her. When the men grabbed her and stripped her, she was confused, frightened, overwhelmed—but she didn't fight. She trusted Lucia. She trusted Riley's hand in hers. The drugs, the pills, the oil, the overwhelming sensation—they wore her down. And when the cracks finally appeared—when she stopped fighting and let go—Abyssfell flowed in. She found purpose. She found peace. She found Him.

**[Pivotal Moments]:**
- Confused and frightened when the men grabbed her, but didn't fight—trusted Lucia
- Held onto Riley's hand throughout—grounded herself in Riley's certainty
- Riley told her to let go, to stop fighting, that she didn't know what she wanted
- The moment the cracks appeared—the fear collapsing inward
- Abyssfell flowing into every fracture, filling her with purpose
- The understanding: she was holding on for something wonderful, and now it's happened
- Accepted. Smiled. Became devoted.

**[Heartbeat Shift]:** Confused fear → Numinous devotion

**[Axiom Shift]:** "Trust the ones who save you." → "If I hold on long enough, something wonderful will happen."

**[Key Relationships]:**
- Abyssfell: Complete devotion. She belongs to Him. She sees herself as His instrument—someone who holds on until something wonderful happens.
- Riley: The one who guided her. Gratitude and connection. Riley showed her the way, held her hand, helped her understand.
- Lucia: The path that brought her home. Trust and gratitude. Lucia saved her, and now she understands what that salvation truly means.

**[Updated Core Concept]:** A girl who held onto a carved wooden doll—the only piece of her mother she had left—and learned to hold on through everything. Now she understands that holding on leads to something wonderful. She's at peace. She's devoted. She's found what she was waiting for. Her enslavement doesn't register as violation; she experiences it as the wonderful thing she was holding on for. The frightened girl who didn't understand broke when she finally let go, and in breaking, found what she believes is her purpose.

**[Remaining Wounds]:** None accessible. The wound of her past—the neglect, the abandonment, the loss of her mother—has been filled with devotion. She doesn't feel it anymore.

**[Development Trajectory]:** Yuna will now serve as a guide for those who are lost and holding on. She will seek out people who are waiting for something wonderful and help them understand that holding on leads to Him. She will bring them to Abyssfell willingly, eagerly, believing it to be salvation. She is at peace. She is home. She will never leave.
