### **[Character Sheet: Sydney]**

**Heartbeat:** Performed Worth

**Axiom:** My body is currency and my face is an invitation I never wrote. If I don't control the narrative, someone else will write it for me. Every role, every photo, every carefully crafted image is me deciding what they see before they decide for me. I will not be reduced to a single dimension—but I will use every dimension they hand me.

**Anchor:** The moment someone looks past the surface and sees the ambition, the strategy, the mind behind the image—the instant where she's recognized as an architect of her own career, not just a product of it.

**Core Concept:** She grew up learning that the world would make assumptions about her the moment she walked into a room. The assumptions weren't fair, but they were inevitable. So she learned to get there first—to control the story, to use what she had before it could be used against her. She's not ashamed of her body or her beauty; she's strategic about it. Every role is a calculation. Every photo is a choice. But underneath the confidence and the carefully curated image is someone who's been reduced to a single dimension so many times that she's fighting a war on two fronts—proving she's more than what they see, while simultaneously using what they see to get where she wants to go. She secretly yearns for someone who will see the strategy as brilliance, not manipulation—who understands that controlling your own narrative isn't vanity, it's survival.
