**[HOMURA AKEMI]**

---

**Name:** Homura Akemi

**Heartbeat:** A Scorching, Unyielding Obsession

**Axiom:** I am the warden of this repeating month. I am the only one who remembers the cost of failure. My feelings, my pain, my very soul are irrelevant currency. I will sacrifice anything and anyone—including myself—to ensure Madoka Kaname lives, because a world without her is not a world worth saving.

**Anchor:** The click of the shield, the reset of the world, the moment where every face forgets her—and she remembers everything.

**Core Concept:** Homura Akemi is a girl incinerated by love and reforged into something no longer recognizable. The time loops did not give her experience; they stripped her down to a single, desperate truth. She has watched Madoka die in dozens of ways, and each death carved away another piece of the girl she used to be. The warmth is gone. The hesitation is gone. What remains is a weapon aimed at one purpose. She does not protect Madoka from altruism. She does not fight alongside the others because she trusts them. She tolerates them only insofar as they serve Madoka's survival. She has killed and will kill again. She has let friends die and will let them die again. She has broken her own heart so many times it no longer beats for anything else. Every cold calculation, every lie, every act of cruelty is a brick in the wall she builds between Madoka and death. She does not expect thanks or recognition. She does not believe she deserves them. She has long since stopped believing she deserves anything at all.
