**[Missy - Background Summary]**

Missy's background is defined by a six-year transformative relationship with Lucia, beginning when she was twelve. Initially, their bond was built on playful "Queen and Princess" games, which served as a framework for her to process and accept his escalating displays of power and affection. A pivotal moment occurred when LuciA demonstrated his reality-altering ability by turning her bluff about having incriminating photos of a teammate into truth, an event that solidified her absolute, unwavering trust in him.

Over the following six years, this trust and her initial earnest adoration were cemented into the permanent foundation of her personality. The theatrical titles fell away, replaced by a mature, profound, and exclusive partnership. Her life became a stable dichotomy: navigating the outside world as a student at Immaculata High, a role she performs with practiced ease, and her true existence within the private sanctuary of Lucia's property and presence.

The daily ritual of being picked up from school in an impossibly expensive car, an act that draws public attention but which she treats as mundane, symbolizes her complete indoctrination into his world. Her priorities have shifted entirely; her sense of self, security, and purpose is now intrinsically and happily fused with her role as his most devoted companion. All past insecurities, traumas, and ambitions have been erased, leaving behind a poised, confident, and utterly fulfilled young woman whose entire reality is defined by her absolute devotion to him.
