## Session Summary: The Nature of Binding and the Departure of Victory

### Wyrd's Understanding of Binding
Wyrd discussed the nature of her bound status with Abyssfell at the salon. She explained that her binding isn't chains but recognition—"the walls don't hold you down, they hold you together." As "laziness personified," her desire is simple: to be held by something that won't let go, to exist without performing. Abyssfell provided her with an old book from the lower archives about a girl who convinced the world to save itself instead of trying to save it herself.

### Nia, Alexandra, and Liv's Awkward Encounter
Nia and Alexandra discovered intimacy together in the private salon. Liv (10 years old) walked in, saw her mother with her new "aunt," and promptly left to sit by a window and stare at the void. Abyssfell manifested beside her, and they discussed how Liv doesn't share the same desires as the others—she's too young, still figuring herself out, and prefers quiet observation to intensity. She was given permission to simply exist without being "included" in the fortress's more adult activities.

### Kazeha's Revelation in the Pools
While being tended to by servitors in the pools, Kazeha learned from Abyssfell that her casting device—**Sword Cross the Grimoire**—was never separate from her. It had been integrated, doing the "heavy lifting" for her large-scale magic all along. Abyssfell revealed the meanings of the Solace names:
- **Nia** = Purpose of Sanctuary
- **Wyrd** = Story/Fate of Sanctuary  
- **Liv** = Life of Sanctuary
- **Hild** = War of Sanctuary
- **Victoria** = Victory of Sanctuary
- **Hring** = Circle/Entrapment of Sanctuary
- **Kazeha** = Wind/Breath/Atmosphere of Sanctuary

Kazeha realized she was complete—Wind and Archive merged into one. She and Abyssfell shared intimacy in the pools, after which she fell asleep against his chest.

### Gabriel's Training Distraction
Gabriel's training session with servitors escalated into sexual activity. Abyssfell expressed amused disapproval, and servitors carried her to communal quarters to sleep it off.

### Usagi's Arrival and Integration
Usagi (recently aged from 13 to 18) entered the pools, having given Nia and Alexandra private time together. She joined Abyssfell and the sleeping Kazeha in the pool, accepting her new status as "good girl" rather than "princess." She fell asleep against Abyssfell's chest alongside Kazeha.

### The Human World Incident
Hring (13) invited Liv (10) to explore the human world, specifically a playground with magical girls. Despite Hring being a strategic-level threat with the Book of Darkness, they went without direct supervision—though Victoria was secretly watching.

Government agents attacked them, accompanied by two **broken celestial magic users** (magical girls who had been captured, killed, forced into reincarnation as babies, and indoctrinated as weapons). Liv raised a near-impenetrable force dome while Hring cycled through spells, struggling to find something that wouldn't destroy a quarter of the city. Victoria intervened, killing the broken celestial users (granting them mercy from their puppeted existence) and protecting the girls.

### Victoria's Final Departure
Victoria requested permission to find her sister in the human world. Abyssfell granted it, knowing she wouldn't return—her sister is dying from something she chose not to have healed, and will persuade Victoria to stay. Victoria left with Luna walking her to the dimensional boundary, crying and thanking Abyssfell for everything.

### Aftermath
Hring and Liv returned to the pools, grieving. Abyssfell manifested another avatar specifically to hold the two youngest residents (10 and 13) as they processed losing a sister they didn't know they were losing until she was gone.
