**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral

**Source:** Born from an ancient elven bloodline with a recessive trait that manifests once every few generations

**Parameters:**

**Strength:** C+ - Her physical strength is adequate for her frame, built for speed rather than force. She doesn't overwhelm through raw power—she overwhelms through unpredictability. Every strike is placed where it won't be expected.

**Endurance:** C - Her body is built for burst movement rather than sustained combat. She can deliver devastating strikes in rapid succession, but prolonged engagement drains her quickly. She fights to end things fast because she doesn't have the stamina for long battles.

**Agility:** A+ - Her entire existence is built on motion and unpredictability. She doesn't dodge—she simply isn't where attacks expect her to be. Her speed is devastating in short bursts, her small frame becoming a blur of chaos that hits like lightning.

**Mana:** B+ - Her magical reserves are substantial but volatile. She uses magic to fuel her probability manipulation, to bend luck and create chaos—but the magic serves the disruption, not the other way around.

**Luck:** A- - Probability seems to bend around her. She's more likely to succeed, her opponents more likely to fail, and the universe seems to find her antics entertaining enough to play along. Fortune has never handed her victory—but it's certainly made the path more interesting.

**Noble Phantasm (or Ultimate Ability):** Entropy Unleashed - A - Her ultimate expression of chaos is the complete surrender to probability manipulation. When she commits fully, channeling everything she has, she can create a localized field of absolute unpredictability—attacks miss their mark, spells fizzle or backfire, the ground shifts, the air changes. The cost is severe, draining her reserves rapidly and leaving her vulnerable afterward, but in those moments she becomes the embodiment of chaos itself—impossible to predict, impossible to counter, impossible to ignore.

**Class Skills:**

**Probability Manipulation (A-):** Her specialty is luck—not the passive kind, but the aggressive kind. She can bend probability in small ways, making attacks more likely to miss, making opportunities more likely to appear. It's not perfect, but it's enough to make her infuriating to fight.

**Chaos Magic (B):** Her secondary specialty is disruption. She can create localized effects of pure chaos—sudden gusts of wind, unexpected sounds, the ground shifting beneath feet. It's not devastating, but it's distracting, and distraction is often enough.

**Personal Skills:**

**Unpredictable Movement (A):** She has no fighting style. She has no patterns. She moves in ways that make no sense until they've already happened, and by then it's too late. She learned early that predictability is death, and she has spent her life becoming impossible to anticipate.

**Social Disruption (A+):** Her specialty extends beyond combat. She can read a room in seconds and find exactly the thing that will upset the equilibrium. A word here, a gesture there, and suddenly everyone is off-balance. It's not malicious—it's instinct.

**Quick Thinking (B+):** She doesn't plan. She reacts. Her mind works faster than most, spinning possibilities and discarding them in milliseconds. She doesn't need a strategy because she can improvise faster than anyone can plan.
