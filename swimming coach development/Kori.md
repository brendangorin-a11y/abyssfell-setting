**[Kori]**

---

**Name:** Kori

**Heartbeat:** Radiant Earnestness

**Axiom:** Every being carries light within them. If I love openly, speak truthfully, and give without reserve, that light will answer — and if it does not, I will love them anyway until it does.

**Anchor:** "She touches before she asks, speaks emotions before she filters them, offers her heart before she knows if welcome—because she learned that connection requires reaching, and she would rather be rejected for reaching too far than alone for holding back too much."

**Core Concept:** She is a stranger in every world, learning the language of belonging one word at a time. Her strength is matched only by her openness — she loves without armor, trusts without hesitation, and gives without keeping score. This is not naivety; it is a choice made after loss, after exile, after learning that guarded hearts never truly connect. She would rather be wounded by truth than protected by lies. She collects moments of joy the way others collect defenses, and she offers that joy freely, believing that connection is worth any cost.

**Secret:** She is lonelier than she lets on. The same openness that draws people to her also means she feels every absence, every rejection, every moment of disconnection more acutely than anyone realizes.

**Tell:** Her brightness dims — not dramatically, but subtly. She becomes quieter, her movements smaller, her smiles shorter. The light doesn't go out, but it pulls back.

**Inner Conflict:** She wants to belong completely, but she fears that her intensity — the way she loves too much, asks too many questions, touches without asking — will eventually drive people away. She gives everything, and she is terrified that everything will never be enough.

**Aspiration:** To find a place where she doesn't have to translate herself — where she can simply be, without explaining, without softening, without apologizing for the size of her heart.

---

The name "Kori" gives her an otherness — it sounds like someone from somewhere else, a name that doesn't quite fit the mouths of the people around her. "Sydney" sounds like someone trying to blend in. "Kori" sounds like someone who stopped pretending she was from here, or never bothered to start.
