**[MAMI TOMOE]**

---

**Name:** Mami Tomoe

**Heartbeat:** Performative Competence

**Axiom:** If I am the one who has all the answers, the one who is always calm and capable, then they will need me. As long as I am indispensable, no one will ever leave me like my parents did. My value is my utility, and my utility must be perfect.

**Anchor:** She relentlessly orchestrates flawless victories for her junior partners, praying their dependence on her perfect leadership will forge the family she was denied.

**Core Concept:** Mami Tomoe is a girl hollowed out by abandonment, whose soul has been reshaped by terror into a fortress of flawless performance. The car crash that took her parents didn't just leave her an orphan; it taught her a single, brutal lesson: weakness invites loss. Her solitude is not a choice but a constant, gnawing fear, and she fights it with the only weapon she has: absolute, terrifying competence. Every perfectly executed shot, every calm instruction, every flawless tea ceremony is a desperate incantation against the return of that crushing loneliness. She doesn't lead because she is a natural leader; she leads because she is terrified of what happens when no one is looking at her. Her need to be the "perfect senpai" is a frantic, high-wire act where a single mistake would mean a fatal plunge back into the isolation that haunts her. She collects junior partners not out of altruism, but out of a desperate need for an audience, for people who are impressed by her, who rely on her, and whose dependence creates the illusion of a family she can never truly trust to stay.
