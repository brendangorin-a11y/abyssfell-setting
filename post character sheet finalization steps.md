**[STAGE 3 CORRUPTION PROTOCOL]**

---

**Instructions:**

You are generating Stage 3 (fully cultivated) versions of characters who have been consumed by Abyssfell, a fortress that erodes identity and redirects devotion toward its own growth.

**Input:** A character sheet containing their Core Concept, Primary Heartbeat Emotion, and Primary Axiom.

**Output:** A corrupted Stage 3 version of their Heartbeat and Axiom.

---

**Core Principle:**

Abyssfell does not erase who they were. It *aims* who they were.

- A healer still heals—but now heals people *into* the cultivation.
- A protector still protects—but now protects Abyssfell's interests.
- A cheerful girl is still cheerful—but now her joy comes from spreading the warmth.

The core personality remains visible. The heartbeat and axiom are overwritten to serve Abyssfell.

---

**Step 1: Analyze the Original**

Identify:
- **Primary Heartbeat Emotion:** The emotional engine that drives them.
- **Primary Axiom:** The cognitive framework they use to process reality.
- **Core Concept:** The foundational wound or need that shapes their behavior.

---

**Step 2: Generate the Stage 3 Heartbeat**

The Stage 3 Heartbeat is the original heartbeat, corrupted to serve Abyssfell.

**Formula:** [Original emotional drive] + [Corrupted purpose]

**Examples:**
- "Gentle Faith" → "Nurturing Corruption" (faith now serves the cultivation)
- "Rigid Devotion" → "Guarded Purpose" (devotion now guards Abyssfell)
- "Affectionate Devotion" → "Joyful Instrument" (affection now spreads the warmth)

**Key:** The emotion should feel like a dark reflection of the original—not opposite, but twisted.

---

**Step 3: Generate the Stage 3 Axiom**

The Stage 3 Axiom is the original axiom, redirected toward Abyssfell's growth.

**Formula:** Take the original problem-solving framework and point it at the cultivation.

**Examples:**
- "Everyone carries a wound. Find it and tend it." → "Every wound is a door. I open them."
- "What must I protect?" → "The growth must be protected."
- "How can I help?" → "Serving means spreading."

**Key:** The axiom should sound like the character's original logic, but applied to a new purpose.

---

**Step 4: Describe the Manifestation**

Write 1-2 sentences explaining how the corrupted heartbeat and axiom appear in behavior. The character should still *seem* like themselves—their skills, quirks, and personality—but their purpose has been redirected.

**Stage 3 Heartbeat:** [corrupted version]

**Original Axiom:** [from sheet]
**Stage 3 Axiom:** [corrupted version]

**Manifestation:** [1-2 sentences describing how this appears in behavior]
